On Jan 14, 2006, around 6:00 PM, Eric, Me, and Miss Tang gathered at Xujiahui, to take a series photos to be published on Trends Travel - the National Geographic partner in China. So here are the selected photo by photographer, Tang Wanli. The article I wrote for the magazine is called "Fall in Love with Shanghai with 6 Years" ([Chinese version of the article](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/cn/20060224_caeaecae.htm)).

Just now, received an invitation for an afternoon tea break this Saturday with old friends from [Linda](http://blog.run2me.com/fish). Suddenly thought about these photos taken 2 years ago. Pretty weird photos, aren't they?
