Found a very good photolog [ziboy.com](http://ziboy.com) today (via [Chinese Tea](http://christina.bizbeez.com)). [Sinosplice](http://www.sinosplice.com/) posted a nice [interview](http://www.sinosplice.com/china/interviews/ziboy.html) with Wen Ling, the owner and photographer of [ziboy.com](http://ziboy.com). The interview was nice since John asked the questions I wanted to ask and Wen's answers were to-the-point too.

![](http://54boy.com/ziliao/DSC09720.JPG)

Credit: Wen Lin from [ziboy.com](http://ziboy.com).

The unique perspective for the city shows us what the city really looks like. The people, the street scene and the signs - all look so familiar but cannot be found anywhere except in his photolog. He really know how to tell a story with his photos.
