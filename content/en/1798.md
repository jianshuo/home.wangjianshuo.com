Last weekend, I organized the team to attend the outdoor training in Shajiabang, Changshu, Jiangsu Province.

On of the interesting practice to challenge one's courage is to jump over a 1.5 meter gap 8 meters above the ground. I jumped.

Pictures below are taken by [York](http://www.bulaoge.com/?york) and [Victor](http://adolfpan.spaces.live.com/).

**About the Trianing**

The training was facilitated by [Shanghai Freely Training](http://www.freelyt.com). The founder, Twins, is an active member in [Smiling Library](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/smiling_library.htm). Very nice person. The training was a huge success. I would highly recommend them if you want to build the team and increase communication between the team members. Blogbus attended the training and [Hengge](http://Hengge.blogbus.com) recommended it to me.
