**Tianzifang**

Tianzifang is at the lanes of Taikang Road. It developed from local residence area, to a new art zone. The Taikan Road is an area where artist gathers in the downtown Shanghai. Then without people even noticed, the lanes along the Taikan Road developed to a new attraction.

As you can see from the picture above, the narrow lanes were laid out, and people enjoy their dinner. Most of the rooms are galleries, and art shops there. It is a very different feel from either the [Xintiandi](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040728_nice_toys_at_xintiandi.htm) (it is too modern), or the [Moganshan Road](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20050409_meet_the_photographer_event.htm) (too artistic).

Here is the [map](http://ditu.google.cn/maps?f=q&source=s_q&hl=zh-CN&geocode=&q=%E7%94%B0%E5%AD%90%E5%9D%8A&ie=UTF8&ll=31.208296,121.468502&spn=0.004395,0.006909&z=17&brcurrent=3,0x35b27a98ca20980d:0xe75dbf2229f58326) of the area.
