Late sleeping last night took effect today. I fall asleep as soon as I sat down at a 10F seat of MU flight. This is the first time in my life that I didn't enjoy a flight - I didn't know when the flight took off and when it landed. All I know is, I sat down, slept, woke up and went home. Amazing! It seems I should stay late every time before a travel thus the boring time during the trip will be easily **skipped**, as if it hadn't existed. Oh. There was even a book at Amazon to teach people to [Sleep on Airplanes](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/tg/detail/-/0972221913/103-9445772-9423037?v=glance). LOL.
