I admire the great achievements New Yorkers created in New York. After I studied New York History at [http://www.nycsubway.org/](http://www.nycsubway.org/), I learnt that the majority of the New York Subway system was completed between 1904 and 1918. With this background information, the current subway seems so advanced and clean. The lines 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, A, B, C, D, E, S, N, Q, R, W, V, L connects the suburbs and Manhattan and make it so convenient.
