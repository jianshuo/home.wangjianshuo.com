I decided to explorer the [degree confluence point](http://www.confluence.org/) of [30N 119E](http://www.confluence.org/confluence.php?lat=30&lon=119). According to the website, it is 7.4 km (4.6 miles) N of Qiuyuan, Zhejiang, China

with approx. altitude of 581 m (1906 ft). I spent some time in the book store to find out the Chinese name for Qiuyuan. It is 秋源.

I will be there in about two week. [Sebastian](http://www.behind-the-wall.org/archives/2003_08.php) has been so kind to alow me to use his GPS for one week. Thanks Sebastian.

**My Resources**

Before I go, I have collected the following resources and it may be of help. (All of them are in Chinese)

[Shangxi Village](http://local.sh.sina.com.cn/yhzn/wanjia1696.html). From the description, I believe the "7.4km north of Qiuyuan" should be some where near Shangxi Village. I checked the two villages and learnt that Shangxi 上西 and Qiuyuan all belong to the Pingmen 屏门, Chunan 淳安 county, Hangzhou, Zhejiang.

Also on [Shangxi](http://www.holley.com.cn/news/details_hot.php?news_id=246). Seems it is a famous place already. There are more than 12 waterfall there.

**[Qiuyuan Map](http://www.multimap.com/map/browse.cgi?X=13262500&Y=3475000&width=700&height=400&client=public&gride=&gridn=&keepicon=false&coordsys=mercator&addr1=&addr2=&addr3=&pc=&advanced=&scale=200000)**

I was surprised that a map company in Euro has so detailed map for China. It is also the only map I found on the Internet to mark Qiuyuan. It is marked as Ch'iu Yüan there. It seems it is the German style translation (note the two dots on u? ?.
