Tonight, I met two nice guests from Six Apart in Shanghai - Ginger Tulley, Six Apart's director of worldwide strategy and analysis, and [Nob Seki](http://www.sixapart.com/about/management), Executive Vice President and CEO of Six Apart in Japan. Thanks Ginger and Seki to send me a Movable Type T-shirt. Ginger asked: "We have T-Shirts with either Six Apart and MovableType. What would you like to have?" Although the actual answer will be both, I chosed the MovableType version, since I will be using MovableType for 3 years in 2 weeks, and my first blog entry ever is [MovableType Successfully Installed on Windows XP](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20020911_movabletype_successfully_installed_on_windows_xp.htm) (on Sept 11, 2002).

![](/archives/2005/08/29/shanghai-movable.type-tshirt.jpg)

MovableType T-Shirt

During the dinner, we talked a lot about the history of Six Apart and the Internet trends in China. Ginger already [told](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20050428_thanks_everyone.htm) me that Six Apart was named after the birthday of the two founders [Mena and Ben](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20050423_i_meet_mena_and_ben_from_six_apart.htm). Their birthdays are just six days apart, so they named their company "Six Apart". I just found out we three were born in the same year. If I'd set up a company with Ben, maybe we should call it "Twenty Six Apart", since I am 26 days younger than Ben.. :-)
