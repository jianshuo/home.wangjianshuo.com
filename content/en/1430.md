These are not cheap hotels. They are among the most expensive hotels in Shanghai. However, I'd like to start the journey of telling the story of "where to stay in Shanghai" with the most recognized names and then to middle level hotels.

**International Branded Hotels**

As [International Branded Hotels in Shanghai (IBHS)](http://cometoshanghai.com) reported, there are 42 international branded hotels in Shanghai (thank [jerry](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20060726_how_many_hotels_in_shanghai.htm#0147) for letting me know the existance of IBHS). This number is obviously not accurate (like they didn't have Hilton on board), but it is good guess. What is "International Branded"? The definition is not clear also.

**Top and Best**
