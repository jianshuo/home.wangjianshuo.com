One of the most significant steps of the promising PubSubHubbub protocol is, Google Reader started to support [PubSubHubbub](http://code.google.com/p/pubsubhubbub/). That means, a post on this blog will appear in Google Reader (and Google Buzz in Gmail) immediately after I click Save.  That is cool. I am happy I finally [enabled PubSubHubbub support](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20100217_enabled_pubsubhubbub_on_my_blog.htm) on this blog the other day.

Again, welcome to the world of real time web.

**Update** *February 21, 2010*

P.S. My test shows, this update appears in Google Buzz about 10 seconds after, but it took about 1 minute to appear in Google Reader. Maybe it is because of cache (enemy of real-time web), or high volume?
