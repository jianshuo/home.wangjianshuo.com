 Picture by Jian Shuo Wang

This picture is taken at Xujiahui. The sun sets down between the [Twin Tower](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040207_twin_tower_rise_in_xujiahui.htm) above the Grant Gateway. No colorful clouds, no relaxing people. Only cementin buildings, like the feeling I described in [Our Lives in Big Gray City](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040311_our_lives_in_big_gray_city.htm).

Check the sunset in Pudong. It is two different worlds in the same city.
