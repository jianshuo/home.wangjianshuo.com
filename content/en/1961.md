*This entry is intended to notify YLFers (Young Leaders Forum) participants about the FYLer gather in Shanghai. Hopefully, many of you have subscribed to the [Young Leaders Forum Fellow Blog Feed](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20071209_young_leaders_forum_fellow_blog_feed.htm), so you should be able to get this news very soon.*

We had a wonderful gather for YLFers. Thanks for the business trip of Rose from Beijing, and organization of Haisong (and paying the bill), we finally had:

- Rose, 2007 Fellow
- Haisong, 2004 Fellow
- Li Hong, 2003 Fellow
- Zhang Yong, 2005 Fellow
- Nick, 2005 Fellow
- Jian Shuo, 2007 Fellow

So nice to see each other again after the [Nanjing trip](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20071205_back_from_wonderful_ylf_trip_in_nanjing.htm). It should be the first known gather after this year's forum.
