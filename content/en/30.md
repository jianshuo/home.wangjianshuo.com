This is a short message from Dao Cheng. After 30+ hours of bus, I finally arrived in Dao Cheng. The Internet access is not easy here. Thanks for Xue Long Zi from [Yading.net](http://www.yading.net) to provide his computer and modem.

One of the 191 pictures I have taken is [here](http://www.wangjianshuo.com/personal/places/daocheng).
