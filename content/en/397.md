**Blogcn.com Shutdown Because of Traffic Reason**

Intead of my guess of media or government pressure, [blogcn.com](http://www.blogcn.com) is not accessible due to the huge volume of the traffic. According to Blogchina.com, blogcn is trying to add more servers to handle the huge traffic. Our reader [JH](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031112_muzimeis_sex_blog_brought_trouble.htm#0242) and [dodo](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031112_muzimeis_sex_blog_brought_trouble.htm#0427), Muzimei's website is accessible on North America while I cannot access it even via proxy in U.S. The traffic reason will explain this - it is not because of the [block](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030708_bugs_in_the_great_firewall.htm), it is because the traffic is much lower at U.S. day time - when the Muzimei seekers in China fall asleep. **"Microsoft Violated My Right of Know"**
