![](/cn/2005/05/08/sanjose-jianshuo-standard.JPG)

 Hi all, welcome to Wangjianshuo's blog. I am Wang Jian Shuo. I live in [Shanghai](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/shanghai.htm). I want to be visitor's personal guide to this amazing city.

I try to help people to [live better in Shanghai](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/livinginshanghai.htm). You can get information about finding a [job](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/jobopening.htm), making [phone calls](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/phonecall.htm), getting [Internet Access](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/internetaccess.htm), using [credit card](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/creditcard.htm) , taking [taxis](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/taxi.htm). I will show you places to [eat](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/placestoeat.htm), to [see](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/placestosee.htm), to [shop](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/placestoshop.htm), or to [buy](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/realestate.htm) :-).

For visitors, I write about [Pudong Airport](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/pudongairport.htm), how to [transit to other places](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/pvgto.htm), via [Maglev](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/maglev.htm), [metro](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/shanghaimetro.htm), [buses](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/bus.htm), or [train](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/travelbytrain.htm).

I introduced some [hotels](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/hotel.htm) to stay, featuring some [cheap and good hotels](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/cheaphotels.htm).

I have an online [Shanghai Map](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/shanghaimap.htm), and accumulated three year of first hand observation of the [weather](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/weather.htm) in this city.

I love the city of Shanghai.  I took [photos](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/shanghaiphotos.htm) to record its [changes](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/shanghaichanges.htm), and am worry about problems like [traffic](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/traffic.htm), or [polution](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/polution.htm)...

I [travelled](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/travel.htm) to many places. I like to visit beautiful places (like [Daocheng](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/daocheng.htm), [Lijiang](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/lijiang.htm), [Sanya](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/sanya.htm), [Xiamen](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/xiamen.htm)), and frequently visit cities like [Beijing](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/beijing.htm), [Guangzhou](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/guangzhou.htm), [Chengdu](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/chengdu.htm) and [Hongkong](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/hongkong.htm). At weekends, I often go to [places near Shanghai](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/nearshanghai.htm). In particular, [Hangzhou](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/hangzhou.htm), [Chongming](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/chongming.htm), [Taihu](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/taihu.htm), [Yangshan](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/deepwaterport.htm), [Shengsi](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/shengsi.htm).

Going abroad, I visited [Seattle](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/seattle.htm), and [San Francisco](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/sanfrancisco.htm) for many times, and went to [New York](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/newyork.htm) and other [east coast cities](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/eastus.htm) for once. On the way there, I ever stayed in [Japan](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/japan.htm) for 5 hours. I hope to complete a *[Visit U.S. Handbook](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/gotous.htm)*. The only thing I don't like for [travel by air](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/travelbyair.htm) is, [jetlag](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/jetlag.htm).

A little bit about myself.

I was [born](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/mybirthday.htm) in [Luoyang](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/luoyang.htm), and went to [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/sjtu.htm). Then I worked in [Microsoft](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/microsoft.htm) for almost 6 years, then joined [Kijiji](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/kijiji.htm) in March, 2005.

I like [sports](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/sports.htm), such as [running](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/running.htm), and [cycling](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/cycling.htm). I am dreaming of having a [boat](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/boat.htm).

Two years ago, I [learnt to drive](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/learntodrive.htm), and bought a [car](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/car.htm). (I call it [Goudaner](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/goudaner.htm)). Then I [moved](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/moving.htm) from Puxi to a [apartment](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/apartments.htm) with [garden](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/garden.htm) in Pudong. Some [cats](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/cats.htm) lives in it.

This [blog](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/blogging.htm) was started more than three years ago. I chose to use [English](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/english.htm) to benefit international visitors. It is now reported by many [media](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/onmedia.htm). I use [MovableType](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/movabletype.htm) as blogging tool, and shared the [tips](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/tips.htm) and the [back stage](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/backstage.htm) to build this [website](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/website.htm). I was forced to change my [hosting](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/hosting.htm) company many times though.

Recently,  I reviewed [companies](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/companies.htm) in [emerging technology](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/newtech.htm) field, attended many [conferences](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/conference.htm), and start to study the [culture](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/culture.htm) of both China and western countries, and see the conflicts when the [west meets the east](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/westmeetseast.htm).

In my life, I also do crazy things. I joined the [confluence project](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/conflence.htm) to visit integer cross point of latitude and longitude, and host [meetup](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/meetup.htm) everywhere. I even visited all [Starbucks](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/starbucks.htm) by walk, and set up an personal photography [exhibition](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/exhibition.htm) in April, 2004.

I enjoy [books](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/books.htm), [movies](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/movie.htm), and trying to understand [art](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/art.htm) and improve my [productivity](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/productivity.htm). I think deep about what [life](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/life.htm) means, how [happiness](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/happiness.htm) works, and how I can [help](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/helping.htm) others. I am proudly a consultant for [Smiling Library](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/smilinglibrary.htm).

I am so happy to have [Wendy](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/wendy.htm) as my wife, to have lots of smart [friends](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/friends.htm), and thousands of [readers](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/myreaders.htm). You can see my collection of [funny](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/funny.htm) [toys](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/mytoys.htm). At this blog, I also celebrate all [holidays](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/holidays.htm), and [special days](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/specialdays.htm) with [good wishes](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/happynewyear.htm). For me, [every single day](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/daylikethis.htm) is a holiday.

Thank you for staying with me.
