Recently, from the family perspective, it is among the toughest time for me and [Wendy](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/wendy.htm).

After entering the 30s, and especially with the newly [arrival](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20070602_my_baby_arrives.htm) of a baby ([Yifan](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/yifan.htm)), many things changed. Combined with the recent [heart surgery](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20071110_hospital_in_shanghai.htm) of Wendy's mother, there are many things happening concurrently. The baby needs to be fed and taken care of, the parents need intensive care. Everyday, Wendy and I am running between home, work place, hospital, and sometimes back to work places - even [car parking](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040327_puxi_is_not_car_friendly.htm) itself is a big problem, not to mention all the meal stuff. Wendy can fall asleep on my shoulder during even a short taxi drive.

I am not complaining at all. It is the time in life that we have to enter. In the Chinese saying: "parents above and children under". Growing older means to be more powerful, and meanwhile, it means more responsibility. It means to give back what you owe parents, and to pass the due responsibility down to the children. It is not easy, but it is warm. It means no [movie](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/movie.htm), no [dining out](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/restaurants.htm), no spare time with [friends](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/friends.htm), or even no time to stay late at office on work. It is another kind of life the we enter into. I suspect this time will last for a long time.

Just as I waved my hands to say good bye to my happy primary school time, and entered into the hot competition in middle school, or I said good bye to my pure life in middle school and entered an aimless [university life](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20070104_shanghai_jiaotong_university.htm), and then kissed good bye to my summer and winter holidays and started to work, or even left the memories of being a single behind me and setup a family, it is the natural process to enter into the next stage in life. There are a lot of fun waiting for me there. I am very grateful that Wendy and I am still young. We still have the power to take our responsibilities.

All we need is time to get used to the new life, and best wishes to my bigger family.

The best is yet to come.
