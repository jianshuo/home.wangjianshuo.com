Have you heard of Google Dance? It is the monthly re-index of all web pages and recaculate [PageRank](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040203_pagerank_higher_position_in_google_result.htm) for each individual web page. Since Google indexes more than 4 billion webpages with more than 10,000 computers, it takes so long to do the full re-index. This is interesting. You may learn more about Google Dance at the following links:

- [Google Dance](http://dance.efactory.de/)

- [Google Dance Tool](http://www.google-dance-tool.com/)

Enough about theory, I just find something interesting on my own website. I can declare that the last Google Dance (for this site) happens on June 6, 2004. Just take a look at this two page:

This page has PageRank of PR2.

![](/archives/2004/07/22/screen-mowing.lawn-google.pr.png)

This page, which is posted one day after has PageRank of PR0.

![](/archives/2004/07/22/screen-successful-google.pr.png)

You get the PR number from the PageRank indicator (right top of the screen) on Google Tool bar - the small green bar.

All pages before [Mowing Lawn](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040606_mowing_lawn.htm) have PR2, PR3 or PR4, and all pages after [Successful Presentation Skill Training](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040607_successful_presentation_skill_training.htm) have PR0.

That means, the 30+ pages will get the PR they deserve (at least PR2 I think) at the next Google Dance. When will it be? I am waiting...
