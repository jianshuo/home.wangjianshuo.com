There is always something interesting outside our homes.

Jin showed me the famous cornfield along the Lawrence expressway in Sunnyvale last night when we drove back to my hotel. At night, I didn't see it so clearly, but I managed to find it out on Google Earth.

Here is a report about the [cornfield](http://www.metroactive.com/papers/metro/09.30.99/cover/best-notions-edit-9939.html).

Joe Francia, the owner, just keep the land and won't sell it. He loves to grow food on his own land. If he stays happy with it, why sell the land for millions of dollars? Interesting guy and gave me a lot inspirations. Thanks Jin to show it to me. BTW, we talked about the land when I asked a silly question yesterday: where are the farm land in Bay Area?

P.S. Google is also very interesting service. I wrote this blog with just a title and a Google Map. I take a snap and when I am up and continued to write this blog, I searched "[Cornfield Sunnyvale](http://www.google.com/search?q=cornfield+sunnyvale&sourceid=navclient-ff&ie=UTF-8&rlz=1B3GGGL_enCN232CN232)" and found this blog entry is already the second result in the search result page. Wow. I want to give credit to Google for searching so fast, and also give my blog some credit for being a reliable content source that Google bot loves.
