Anyone here ever visited Dubai? Saw the Dubai Tower?

![](/archives/2009/03/10/2089584920_293387ca50.jpg)

Image in courtesy of [Ammar Abd Rabbo](/photos/21499556@N04/)

I saw an article about Dubai on the recent issue of *[Asia Business Leaders](http://www.ableaders.com)*. I was very impressed by the Dubai Tower. It is so tall. When you see things as tall as this big, people may give up the idea of building a even taller building than that.

At least, I lost my interest in the future Shanghai Tower (632m when finished), which is taller than the current Shanghai [World Financial Center](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20080115_world_financial_center.htm), but much lower than this tower.

I admit that people sometimes get crazy about building really tall architect, and be very passionate about it, until you see some really really tall things, and ops... why bother doing that?
