What a pleasant surprise for me that I received email from Dr. Glenwood Irons  again. you may also remember Dr. Glenwood, the author of [Flight to Shanghai: A Journey in the Plague Year](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030504_flight_to_shanghai_a_journey_in_the_plague_year.htm) ([II](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030505_flight_to_shanghai_a_journey_in_the_plague_year_ii.htm), [III](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030510_flight_to_shanghai_a_journey_in_the_plague_year_iii.htm), [IV](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030512_flight_to_shanghai_a_journey_in_the_plague_year_iv.htm)). It was the only time that I co-author this blog for the entire 3 years of blogging, because the topic is so interesting and Dr. Glenwood's entry was so well written. This time, he sent me some pictures he took near the [Lugu Lake](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20050501_lugu_lake.htm). He obviously took the most moving moments - dancing and group photo with the women in that village - the villages where women enjoy much more rights than men.
