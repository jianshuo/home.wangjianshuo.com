I was not happy: [ODP - Not As Good as Zeal.com](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20020914_odp_not_as_good_as_zealcom.htm). (ODP ~= DMOZ). I was happy: [This Site Listed in DMOZ](http://wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031107_wendy_became_pmp_and_this_site_listed_in_dmoz.htm).

Today, I saw a frequent commentor on my Chinese blog, Xiaoma, is a DMOZ editor for [blog category](http://www.google.com/Top/World/Chinese_Simplified/%E8%AE%A1%E7%AE%97%E6%9C%BA/%E4%BA%92%E8%81%94%E7%BD%91%E7%BB%9C/%E7%BD%91%E7%BB%9C%E8%B5%84%E6%BA%90/%E5%8D%9A%E5%AE%A2/%E4%B8%AA%E4%BA%BA/?il=1) (Chinese). It is nice. He is the first DMOZ editor I know.

In the last month, rumor said "ZEAL.com is to be closed". Today, rumor [Google Is Banning Sites That Use Open Directory (DMOZ) Data](http://www.webmasterworld.com/forum30/33761.htm).

It seems both project is at its hard stage now. Anyway, I quickly submitted my Chinese blog to the Chinese blog category. My previous submission experience was really bad - just as they explained in their help document, it may takes months for a site to get reviewed. In my case, over 1 year...
