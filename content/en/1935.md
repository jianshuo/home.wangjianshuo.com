Wendy and I was stuck in the middle of a traffic jam for about 10 minutes. It is due to the continued [diesel shortage](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20071028_0_gas_shortage_in_shanghai.htm) in Shanghai. Large trucks are partying on the road - long lines of trucks lined up from the other gas station (the one on the opposite side of [station which just exploded yesterday](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20071124_gas_station_explosion_near_my_home.htm)) to the corner of the road and winding toward the road leading to my home direction. They occupied the right lane, so jammed the traffic for some time.

Is it a sign for future problems?
