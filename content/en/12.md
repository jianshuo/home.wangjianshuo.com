I came across a very [interesting site](http://www.spartaneity.com/) yesterday by reference of [andersja's blog](http://www.jacobsen.no/anders/blog/). There is a page named [Freedom to Reproduce Content?](http://www.spartaneity.com/02/09/freedom_to_reproduce_content.html), I found [another blog](http://www.blogroots.com/comments.blog/157) with 72 comments. Wow. It is a very popluar web site. Finally, I found it is the web site for the famous weblog book - "[Web Blog: Publishing Online with Weblogs](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0764549626/ref%3Dase%5Fblogroots-20/104-7974453-1919137)".

In [chapter 3](http://www.blogroots.com/chapters.blog/id/8), the author mentioned [Blogger Code](http://www.leatheregg.com/bloggercode/). It is interesting that someone over there are developing different strange [tools](http://www.blogchalking.tk/)and codes to claify bloggers. It is very cool and interesting.

Here is my blogger code

> B1 d++ t+ k s+ u-- f+ i- o+ x-- e+ l c

You can use [this decoder](http://travis.kroh.net/archives/blogger_decoder.html) to decode it.
