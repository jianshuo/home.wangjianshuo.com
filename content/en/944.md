[Eric](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/mvm) sent me a link early morning to ask me check a [photo website on Xinjiang](http://www.bluezone.cn/diary/xinjiang/). The pictures are very good - I especially love the photos in Shanghai which reveals exactly the feeling I have on Shanghai. The story is quite long, as Eric "complained", and I didn't go into the Xinjiang scene part yet. If the author want to provide a thumbnail view, it would be very nice.

In Xinjiang, there are scenes like [this](http://www.bluezone.cn/diary/xinjiang/021.htm). China has a diversed culture: The

[Bar](http://www.bluezone.cn/diary/xinjiang/017.htm), the [cups](http://www.bluezone.cn/diary/xinjiang/019.htm), the [musical instrument](http://www.bluezone.cn/diary/xinjiang/032.htm), the [people](http://www.bluezone.cn/diary/xinjiang/057.htm), the [road](http://www.bluezone.cn/diary/xinjiang/102.htm), the [house](http://www.bluezone.cn/diary/xinjiang/103.htm), the [field](http://www.bluezone.cn/diary/xinjiang/104.htm)... It is all so different.

BlueZone, very nice job!

I planned the [Kanas trip](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030527_travel_plan_to_kanas_lake_xinjiang.htm)but still cannot fullfil the plan. Eric and [Dan](http://shanghaidiaries.com) went there with very nice pictures. Now, I feel my passion to go to the wild and far areas is not as strong as before. Why?
