Went to see Zhangyimou's film Shi Man Mai Fu (or House of Flying Daggers) in the theator of [Super Brand Mall](http://www.wangjianshuo.com/personal/places/shanghai/sbm/superbrandmall.htm).

![](/archives/2004/07/24/screen-house.of.flying.daggers-v.jpg)

Image in courtesy of [Sina.com.cn](http://ent.sina.com.cn/download/photo/smmf.html)

I don't have too much comments on this film. A perfect film on visual effects but so-so in the story.

![](/archives/2004/07/24/shanghai-theater-seats.jpg)

Image in courtesy of the Theater at SBM

![](/archives/2004/07/24/shanghai-theater-sbm.jpg)

Image in courtesy of the Theater at SBM

Most of Shanghai's theater has been equiped with cutting-edge audio, video and environment facilities. It is seldom see "large theater halls". As shown on the pictures, the hall is not large - I was sitting at the left most seat of the last row.
