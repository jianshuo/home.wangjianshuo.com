My friend is in bad mood these days. His visa application to U.S. was rejected so he cannot attend [TechEd 2004 in San Diego](http://www.microsoft.com/seminar/teched2004/default.mspx). This is the second time he missed the chance due to Visa problems.

Visa is one of the biggest [barriers for people to go out of the country](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030705_back_from_culturexchina_party.htm). I heard it is due to SARS, but it is not confirmed. There is no report on this on major international media, but many of them reported "[Illegal US visa call centre closed](http://www.voanews.com/article.cfm?objectID=3E0EF1D6-DC07-407F-8754FA1A8296A50D)".

Maybe due to security concerns, the United States Consulate in Shanghai has replaced the tall gate with tall walls at the interaction of Huai Hai Rd. and Wulumuqi Rd. Now there is only a small gate at Wulumuqi Rd. now.
