[Run](http://http://blog.run2me.com/runliu), [Linda](http://blog.run2me.com/fish) and Jack visited my home (to be more exact, my garden) this afternoon. Happy about that. The Sun of spring shines perfectly and we were happy to eat banana (my favorite), and apple ([Wendy](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/fanfan/)'s favorite) under my big umbrella.
