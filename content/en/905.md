What I did today:

- Amtrak from [New Rochelle](http://www.newrochelleny.com/) to Grand Central. The running time was exactly 35 minutes.

- From Grand Central to World Trade Center Site. The new [Freedom Tower](http://www.cnn.com/2003/US/Northeast/12/19/wtc.plan/) is under construction already.

- Visited Wall Street. I didn't expect the most important street in financial world is so short. Due to the Christmas day, I didn't see a single person on the street with suite and tie. Actually, I didn't see many people there at all.

- Heading south, I reached the Hudson River but didn't take ferry to the Statue of Liberty.

- Took Metro Line 5 and reached 86 st. Station. Then visited the [Metropolitan Museum of Art](http://www.metmuseum.org/). The most important exhibition there happened to be *[China: Dawn of a Golden Age, 200-750 AD](http://www.metmuseum.org/special/China/index.asp)*. Many of the selections were found in [Luoyang](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030131_luoyang_henan_tour.htm), my hometown.

- Finally, we took M1 to the New York Public Liberty and

- Went along the 42nd st. to Times Square.

- Then went along the 50th st. to [Rockefeller Center](http://www.rockefellercenter.com/home.html). The huge Christmas tree is there and there are "people mountains and people seas".

- We went to the Chinatown via Metro 6 (Canel st.) and had a wonderful dinner.

- Went back to Grand Central and took Amtrak back to New Rochelle. It ran for 31 minutes.
