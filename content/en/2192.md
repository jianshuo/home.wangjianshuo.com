Business is an interesting game.

It is like online game, with strategies playing a role, with execution, with resources, with consistency, with strong minds, with sense of directions, with changes we have to make every day... It is just like a wonderful, interesting, powerful, but complicated game.

Business is really a game.

P.S. This is the fourth entry I wrote with exactly the same title: Business is Business.

The first time was in 2002: [Business is Business](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20021211_business_is_business.htm). That was just before I started to run the ESS business for Wicresoft.

The second time was in July of 2007: [Business is Really Business](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20070719_business_is_really_business.htm). That was a short summary of what business means to me.

The third time was in Oct 2007. [Business is Business](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20071012_business_is_business.htm), but at that time, the term business really mean finance for me.

Today, the "business" does mean business.
