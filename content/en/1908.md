Wendy, her parents, Yifan and I went to the Donghai bridge today. It is the second time for me to get onto the bridge, and the first time for others. Maybe is the second bridge Yifan has ever been to (with the first one as Nanpu Bridge).

You can check my previous entry on [Donghai Bridge](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20050717_donghai_bridge.htm) to know the background of this bridge.

To be short, it is the longest sea bridge in the world with 32.5 km on the sea, and another about 10 km on the land or the [Yangshan Deep Water Port](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20060420_visited_yangshan_deep_water_port.htm).

This time, I took my camera with me so I can show you a little bit of the bridge, and the island.
