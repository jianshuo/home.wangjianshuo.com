To be short, 1463 km (or 909.066054 miles).

This is according to the railway distance. If you take [train from Shanghai to Beijing](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040801_train_from_beijing_to_shanghai.htm), you can arrive there within 13 hour.

For flight, it is 1033 km.
