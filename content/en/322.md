>

>
> Posted by: mvm on August 20, 2003 03:24 PM

He is definitely right. The most famous job posting websites in Shanghai are

1) [http://www.51job.com](http://www.51job.com)

![](/archives/2003/08/20/screen-51job-logo.gif)

2) [http://www.zhaopin.com/](http://www.zhaopin.com/)

![](/archives/2003/08/20/screen-zhaopin-logo.gif)

3) [http://www.chinahr.com](http://www.chinahr.com)

![](/archives/2003/08/20/screen-chinahr-logo.gif)

They are designed mainly for local people. The posting and the interface is in Chinese. Even if you search on an English website, the majority of the results are in Chinese.

You will also find the [forums](http://shanghaiexpat.com/XForum+main.html) on ShanghaiExpat.com helpful. The users of this forum are all expats and there are some good job offers there. Meanwhile, I also opened an job posting forum on my site.
