Big congratulations to Wendy. She successfully [passed](http://www.chinapmp.net/docc/8-2.htm) the PMP ([Project Management Professional](http://pmi.org/prod/groups/public/documents/info/pdc_pmp.asp)) Exam and became a one of the 50,000 PMP worldwide (according to [PMI](http://pmi.org)). I know this is not an easy job. She prepared for it for about 4 months and took the exam the day before our wedding. :-D

Hehe. Very good news for me too. I am so happy also.

**Wangjianshuo.com is (Finally) Listed in DMOZ**

Today, when I checked the server log, I found a huge jump in hit rate. They are refered from the [Regional > Asia > China > Provinces and Regions > Shanghai > Travel and Tourism](http://directory.google.com/Top/Regional/Asia/China/Provinces_and_Regions/Shanghai/Travel_and_Tourism/) in DMOZ directory. I tried to become an editor but was [rejected](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20020914_odp_not_as_good_as_zealcom.htm). I'd like to thank the current editor for the listing.

There is only 7 sites in the list today and it explains the reason it attracts more visit. :-) Interesting!

**One Word Keyword**

By checking the referer log, I found my site can be discovered by some keywords that is so popular. Maybe you will be surprised by the result as I was. I only list single-word keywords where my site was listed in top 10 result.

**[WANG](http://www.google.com/search?hl=en&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&q=wang)** - one of the most popular last name in China (also should be the among the top popular last names in the world providing the large China population).

**[Tongli](http://www.google.com/search?sourceid=navclient&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&q=tongli)** - my favorite old town.

**[Dopod](http://www.google.com/search?sourceid=navclient&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&q=dopod)** - The hottest smartphone in China.

**[Daocheng](http://www.google.com/search?hl=en&lr=&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&q=daocheng)**

**[Pudong](http://www.google.com/search?sourceid=navclient&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&q=pudong)**

**[Shanghainese](http://www.google.com/search?sourceid=navclient&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&q=shanghaiese)**

Actually, some very popular keywords with two words (such as [Sony P8](http://www.google.com/search?sourceid=navclient&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&q=sony+p8) and [MSN Emotion](http://www.google.com/search?sourceid=navclient&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&q=msn+emotion)) also hit the site.
