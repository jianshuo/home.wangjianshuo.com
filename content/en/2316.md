Who gets my help today?

> This is my first time travelling in Shanghai. I wanted to know how we can go to JW Marriott Tomorrow Square at Nanjing West Road.
>
> Will it be possible for me to take the Maglev Train to theJW Marriott hotel in Nanjing West Road.

The answer is Yes, you can take Maglev to [JW Marriott Tomorrow Square Hotel](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031028_jw_marriott_in_tomorrow_square_opens.htm).

Here is the route:

1. Take Maglev at Pudong Airport (here is [how](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031021_pvg_location_of_maglev_station.htm)).

2. Transit to Metro Line #2 at Long Yang Road Station (here is [how](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040424_maglev_at_long_yang_station.htm))

3. Get off the train at [People's Square Station](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20080108_shanghai_metro_peoples_square_station.htm). You'd better take the Nang West Road Exit.

4. The hotel is immediately at the exit. It is located somewhere in the middle of Nanjing West Road Station, and People's Square Station of Metro Line #2. You may need to walk about 10 minutes to get to the hotel. However, you can see the hotel immediately when you leave the station - because it is now the tallest building in Puxi area in Shanghai.

If you take taxi, it should be around 140 RMB.

Happy Travelling!
