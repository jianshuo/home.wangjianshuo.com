Shanghai is cold these days - it is actually freezon. Affected by the cold air rushing from north, Shanghai reaches the lowest temperature of this year - about -3 degrees C. It is a really rare whether in Shanghai. I cannot get used to that cold so far.

This reminds me of the first year I came to Shanghai. That is in 1995 and I just entered [Shanghai Jiaotong University](http://www.sjtu.edu.cn). It is located far away from the downtown Shanghai and is 2-3 degrees colder than in downtown. That was really a hard time for me.
