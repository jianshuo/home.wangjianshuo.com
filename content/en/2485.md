[Robert Mao](http://robertmao.com) posted a [nice article](http://robertmao.com/2009/04/29/teddybear/) talking about two outer space projects.

One is the [Teddy Bear project](http://www.dailymail.co.uk/sciencetech/article-1091896/Out-world-British-teddy-bears-strapped-helium-weather-balloon-reach-edge-space.html). The pupils in UK made the balloon and shipped it with two teddy bears, and send it to 30 km away from space.

![](http://i.dailymail.co.uk/i/pix/2008/12/04/article-1091896-02B07D16000005DC-691_634x476.jpg)

I love this picture also - the pupils put a teddy bear into a plastic bottle to keep it warm in the harsh cold in outer space, and another one wear a plastic head cover - seems to be the bottom of a Pepsi bottle.

![](http://i.dailymail.co.uk/i/pix/2008/12/04/article-1091896-02B14C99000005DC-982_634x431.jpg)

I talked with Wendy about this project for a long time, and I do wish we can create the same project today ourselves, to explore the outer space by ourselves. Wendy said: "Sometimes tech can make people blind of what they can do." I agree. Anyone want to join?
