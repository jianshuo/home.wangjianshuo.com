Max dropped an email to us: [Google blacklists BMW.de](http://news.com.com/Google+blacklists+BMW.de/2100-1024_3-6035412.html?tag=sas.email) this morning.

Two points I saw in this post and [Cutts' blog](http://www.mattcutts.com/blog/):

1. They ([Google](http://Google.com)) have webspam team who do the removal MANNUALLY.

2. They think JavaScript redirect and presenting "too" different view for robot and to users are not good, as specified in their [webmaster quality guidelines](http://www.google.com/webmasters/guidelines.html#quality).
