I saw two astronauts under the big ball of Metro City in [Xujiahui](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030811_shanghai_scenery_around_xujiahui.htm). It is around 6:30 PM. It is almost dark and the area become very crowded. These two men wear silver clothes and black glasses and was walking around without any emotion on their faces. There is a large LCD display on their back. Of cause, they are very eye catching.

![](/archives/2004/01/12/shanghai-astronaut.ad-back.jpg)

![](/archives/2004/01/12/shanghai-astronaut.ad-walking.jpg)

![](/archives/2004/01/12/shanghai-astronaut.side-walking.jpg)

So interesting. I thought they were advertising for LCD. It turned out that they are advertising for the Xuhui Police Office. The characters were so small that I don't know what they wanted to say. It is said they will appear in Xujiahui from 5:30 to 9:30 PM.
