If you have me on your MSN contact list, you know that I seldom change my display name from the formal one: "Jian Shuo Wang". Recently, after reading Wendy's articles, I cannot help putting her beautiful sentences into my MSN Messenger display name. The last display was:

![](/archives/2004/06/19/screen-msn.jianshuowang-happiness.png)

I cannot translate her sentences into English well, since they are just so beautiful in Chinese. A rough idea is: The hint of happiness gradually showed up. It is take from [this article](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/fanfan/20040613_63.htm).

Tonight, I changed display name to *Go mawing in the lovely garden*.

![](/archives/2004/06/19/screen-msn.jianshuowang-garden.PNG)

This one was taken from her article [Free Saturday](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/fanfan/20040619_67.htm)

Wendy is a talented writer. Her article in Chinese is sweet, decent and delightful. Among all the readers, I am, of cause, the most enjoyed reader. This afternoon, she was a little bit worried: Why people don't like to put comment on my weblog? Is anybody reading it? I am sure many people are reading [her blog](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/fanfan). Why not leave some comments to make her happy? :-D (Sorry. There is only Chinese version available.)
