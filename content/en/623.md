In additional to the existing [6 lines of airport shuttle buses](http://www.wangjianshuo.com/personal/places/pudongairport/Bus.htm), a new airport bus line started operation.

**Time Table**

At Dong Fang Rd.

First bus: 6:30

Last bus: 21:40

At Pudong Airport

First Bus: 7:50

Last Bus: 23:00

**Price**

Ticket price starts from 2 RMB.

**Bus Stops**

- Dongfang Rd.

- Pudian Rd.

- Centuary Park

- Longyang Rd. Metro Station

- Keyuan Rd.

- Zhangjiang Nong-gong-shang super market

- Chengxi

- Chuansha bus stop

- [Pudong airport](www.wangjianshuo.com/personal/places/pudongairport/)
