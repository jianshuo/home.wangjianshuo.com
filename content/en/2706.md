We started technical days with a great name - Lego Day. Yes. The Lego means the Denmark toy manufacture [Lego](http://Lego.com).

We bought [Lego 66284 Build and Play Value Pack](http://item.taobao.com/auction/item_detail.jhtml?item_id=c757f233efb3e47688232e4768b70b0d&x_id=0db2) that consists of [5573](http://shop.lego.com/Product/?p=5576&cmp=afc-bp6648365778&hqs=5573)and [5576](http://shop.lego.com/Product/?p=5576&cmp=afc-bp6648365778&hqs=5576).

Among all the bricks, obviously, LEGO 3001 is the star. A simple brick that is manufactured the most, and most easily found in every corner of this world. It is the spirit of create something and make it really good, instead of creating many different functions but none of them is as classic as Lego 3001 (I am talking about website design and almost all business).
