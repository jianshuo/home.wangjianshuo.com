I am trying to migrate [Wendy's blog](http://wangjianshuo.com/fanfan/)  from MovableType 3.2 to 4.2 Beta, however, after about an hour, I still cannot import the files into the new system. So...  I have to get back to MovableType 3.2.

So far, I still believe MT 3.2 is the best blogging software on the earth - simple, fast, and reliable. I don't like MT 4.0, I don't like 4.1, and now I don't like MT 4.2 Beta
