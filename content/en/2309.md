I am at gate 31 of Beijing Airport, heading to [Hongqiao Airport](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20041127_hong_qiao_sha_vs_pu_dong_pvg_airport.htm) in Shanghai. The two day Beijing trip was full of fun and inspiration. Among my trip, talk with Kooxoo.com founder [Chen Hua](http://dawnli.spaces.live.com/), and talk in Baidu were highlight.

When I left Beijing, I just feel if you ask me about the landscape of cities in China, and in US, I would even say the current Beijing is more westernized (or modernized).
