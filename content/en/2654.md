This is the photo of the 100 Century Ave on the 91th floor of Shanghai World Financial Center. I [wrote about it](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20091003_get_to_top_of_swfc_within_100_rmb.htm) few days ago. Here are photos.

It is a very good place to look at the top of Jinmao Tower.

![](/archives/2009/10/09/3995490974_4dcaa87475.jpg)

A little bit northwards. I was surprised that I can actually see the sea, and the Chong Ming Island from the tower.

![](/archives/2009/10/09/3994730325_eac0b8738c.jpg)

This is inside the cafe - tall roof, but pretty crowded - limited seats are available.
