After using [Sony CX500E](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20091011_finally_bought_sony_cx500e.htm) for a while, I have to say, I am extremely satisfied with this camcorder. Just like the Sony P8 camera, this Sony product is perfect, and I always feel I am lucky to choose it. There are several reasons.

- The SteadyShot feature is amazing. I can take video when I am walking, even running a little bit, but the final result is just like the camera were mounted to a tripod - very clear.

- The 12X zoom is wonderful, and useful.

- The bright image it captures. It is much better than the dark image of Nikon.

- I didn't have any expectation for the software coming with any hardware, but the PMB (Photo Motion Browser) was wonderful, even better than Picasa.

- The nightshot is a must for me - to record Yifan at night when Wendy and I was telling story to him at night

- The camera is relatively small to carry. With CX500E, I don't need to carry cameras at all.

- The big LCD screen and touch screen feature - wonderful

I have recorded many video (12G of them). I still didn't figure out a way to share them yet - I don't feel comfortable to upload it to [Tudou.com](http://Tudou.com), or [Youku.com](http://Youku.com). YouTube is blocked in China - troublesome for me. Will figure it out later.

If you are still choosing which camcorder to buy, I highly recommend this camera: Sony CX500E.
