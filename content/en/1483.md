Went to see The Banquet by Zhang Ziyi. The movie was directed by Feng Xiaogang...

It was 35 RMB at 10:20 PM at the Cinema of Grand Brand Mall.

I don't have too many comments for this movie. It is not as interesting as other Feng Xiaogao movies, but it is not as bad as *Wuji*...

From the logical analysis perspective, I feel although the director changed, the style is not too much different from the previous several movies, like [House of Flying Daggers](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040724_house_of_flying_daggers_or_shi_mian_mai_fu.htm), or Wuji. The team (the photographer, creative designers, music, and act designer) are the same as other Chinese movies. Now, the scene is good, but the story is not as strong. It takes time for the staging and story to catch up, and it will be really a great movie.

![](/archives/2006/09/16/screen-the.banquet.jpg)

Image in courtesy of *The Banquet*

Zhang Liangying (one of the winner of the Super Girl) performed very well in the ending song. It is one of the highlights for the movie.
