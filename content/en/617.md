Tour to Shanghai? What you need to know about the city? I have spent two years working on this topic and now, I want to look back and give people some handy link to the most important stuff you must know before you set your foot to this city.

**Air Ticket to Shanghai**

Booking international flight from country in the world to Shanghai is simple. To book flight from Shanghai to other cities in China is not easy if you are outside China. Don't try large booking system like [Expedia.com](http://www.expedia.com) - it suggests you to transit at San Francisco if you check for a flight from Shanghai to Beijing. :-D Check [China Domestic Flight Booking Guide](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030714_pvg_book_domestic_flight_in_china.htm) this page for more information.

**Airport**

If you take international flights to Shanghai, you must be landing at [Shanghai Pudong Airport](http://www.wangjianshuo.com/personal/places/pudongairport/). There is another international airport, [Shanghai Hongqiao Airport](http://www.wangjianshuo.com/personal/places/hongqiaoairport/). It is used for backup to Pudong Airport on international flight. It mainly serves domestic flight. You may use this one if you transit to other China cities.

**Money**

Only Chinese RMB is accepted in Shanghai. Exchange your money after you arrive at [airport](http://www.wangjianshuo.com/personal/places/pudongairport/Currency.htm) or visit a local [bank](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/links/Shanghai/Bank/).

**Mobile**

[Bring your Mobile and Internet to Shanghai.](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040105_bring_your_mobile_and_internet_to_shanghai.htm)

You will like foods in Shanghai. Most of my foreign friends like foods here. I have a list of [restaurant](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/links/Shanghai/Bars/).

[Taxi](http://www.wangjianshuo.com/personal/places/shanghai/taxi.htm), [Metro](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031104_shanghai_metro_map_and_timetable.htm) and [Bus](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030915_scrawl_on_shanghai_buses.htm) are major transportation methods.

Didn't reserved a hotel yet? Here is my [hotel guide](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030815_shanghai_hotel_guide.htm)

**Language**

People in Shanghai speak [mandarine](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031003_mandarin_or_shanghaiese.htm). Local people speak [Shanghainese](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040411_learn_shanghainese.htm). Young people can speak [English](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20021114_is_english_skill_that_important.htm).
