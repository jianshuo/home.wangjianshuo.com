It is prooven that professional web hosting like [iPowerWeb](http://www.ipowerweb.com) is really better than the hosting environment at my [home](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20020925_homewangjianshuocom_moved_out_of_my_home.htm) :-D and better than my previous [hosting company](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040331_byebye_companycn.htm). I was suffering for server down [[1](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030625_server_down_again.htm), [2](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030418_resumed_from_the_short_pause_of_service.htm), [3](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040223_server_down_and_up.htm), [4](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20020924_server_outage_my_home_is_still_not_a_perfect_datacenter.htm), [5](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040329_sorry_for_server_outage.htm)] at the begining of 2004. Then I migrated the server to professional web hosting service. The cost doubled but the result is better than tripled. I checked the availiablity report today and the result is not bad:

![](/archives/2005/02/22/screen-home.wangjianshuo.com-uptime.png)
