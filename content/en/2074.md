During the SARS period in 2003, I wrote an article [Protect China - Not Only From SARS](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030425_protect_china_not_only_against_sars.htm). Just as I am not a big fan of CCTV, I am not a fan of the CNN or BBC. I am not new to incorrect media report from western media, especially BBC (I was quoted by them many times, and the last time was like [this](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20051107_bbcs_interview.htm)).

As far as I remember, the last time BBC quote what I said was on its Chinese homepage: "[Chinese Netizen: We can Say Whatever We Want to Say](http://news.bbc.co.uk/chinese/simp/hi/newsid_4460000/newsid_4461200/4461232.stm)", and in the article, they said:

> Chinese famous blogger Wang Jian Shuo accepted BBC's English interview after the Chinese Blogger Conference. He believe the journalist misunderstood his meaning.
> He criticized BBC for getting words out of contexts, use the edited sentence to get a misleading conclusion.
> He pointed out, that even though you said "no comment", BBC will say "You are under political pressure (and don't want to comment)".
> Wang Jian Shuo also wrote on his blog: "Obviously, this is no censorship on this blog, and I can say whatever I want"

The [original blog](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20051107_bbcs_interview.htm) is there untouched since published, and find out the whole picture. I quitted the discussion with BBC, since one is using the power of a strong media, and I am fighting with my small blog. I am sure my name is in their database, and I am often called to ask for live radio broadcast of BBC, which I all rejected.

**More Errors**

Not only BBC. Many media love to cut things out of context - pretty understandable in news report due to limitation of page, but if you use the cut version to tell a different story, that is another thing.

I am sharing some screen shot created by netizens in China, and I quoted from [anti-cnn.com](http://anti-cnn.com/). I am not sure why but everyone seems like Nepal police more and all use their photos to report what is happening in Tibet.
