I am a fan of pi. I once can remember a lot of digits of pie, but now, I can only remember the following, using the segmentation when I recite it:

3.1415926

535

8979

2884

This is roughly the first 85 digits of pie. Yes. I recite it when I write it.

When [Yifan](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/yifan.htm) grows up, I will be sure to **suggest** (yes, only suggest) him to try to recite the first 200 digit of pie. It is a pretty interesting thing to recite. I also tried to recite e, but just found out e is not as interesting as pi. If Yifan don't want to do that, I can cook a pie for him.
