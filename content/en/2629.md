The car plate bidding this morning resulted in the lowest bidding price to be 27200 RMB for one car plate in Shanghai ([news source](http://sh.sina.com.cn/news/s/2009-09-19/1204117894.html))

I kept tracking the [car plate](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/car.htm) since [2003](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030824_a_car_plate_38500_rmb_in_shanghai.htm). I wanted to buy a car plate at the very beginning, and then choose a [Hangzhou plate](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040920_cars_with_non-shanghai_plates.htm). Now, Wendy and I started to think about a Shanghai plate. We planned to participate in the bidding this month, but just didn't take action to pay the required 2000 RMB bidding deposit. Maybe the next month.
