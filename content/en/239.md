**My digital camera**

You know what, finally, I bought Sony DSC-P8 today. I am [choosing](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030520_starbucks_in_shanghai.htm) between Sony P8 and Nikon E4300. Thanks for [Christina](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030520_starbucks_in_shanghai.htm#0959) from [Chinese tea](http://christina.bizbeez.com) and [Li Jinyi](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030520_starbucks_in_shanghai.htm#0323) to give me the suggestions. I feel a little bit embrassed that although Li is suggesting that Sony is so-so, I still paid for it and get one.

**Nikon is good, expect the Purple Fringing**

I like Nikon since it sounds more professional - actually it is profession in terms of image quality and the lens. But it is completely unacceptable for the [Purple Fringing](http://www.dpreview.com/reviews/nikoncp885/page15.asp).  Check [the](http://www.dpreview.com/reviews/nikoncp885/Samples/Fringing/010825-1700-10-002.jpg) [photos](http://www.dpreview.com/reviews/nikoncp885/Samples/Fringing/DSCN1604-002.jpg) on dpreview.com. I found the same thing in many Nikon 885 photos on many other sites.

**I am excited to have Sony DSC-P8**

I proudly announce that I have digital camera now and I can add more pictures of Shanghai into this blog. My plan includes a series of photo for Starbucks - according to [Luo](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030520_starbucks_in_shanghai.htm#1032), there are 26 Starbucks in Shanghai.

Many other friends also suggested me to take something and put it online, for example, the new Suzhou rivier, the new Lupu bridge... a lot of pictures. Now I can show more rich and informative Shanghai guide soon.

Nice to [hear](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030519_sars_almost_goes_to_an_end.htm#0242) from Annie. She is at the Pudong airport and she is able to access this webpage! Isn't great. Read [her comment](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030519_sars_almost_goes_to_an_end.htm#0242) and check out how she did it.

**Update**: *October 07, 2005*

After two years, I still enjoy Sony P8 and took thousands of pictures and post it onto this site. It is a very cute camera. The best part is, it does not require any driver to transfer photos to a computer.
