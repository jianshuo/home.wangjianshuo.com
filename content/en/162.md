In this month, there is a piece of breaking news - **golarger** spam. They complained and posted [here](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030227_golargercom_net_send_spamer_and_comment_spammer.htm#Comments282) and [there](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030228_fight_back_for_golarger_spam.htm#Comments317).
