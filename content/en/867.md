George sent me the wallop invitation  today. Thanks very much!

Wallop is Microsoft's answer to blog, social software... It is distributed as GMail way - only those who have the invitation can login to Wallop.

This is my way to distribute the invitation ticket I got (inspired by [Run](http://www.run2me.com)).

Below are quoted from [http://bbs.wangjianshuo.com/ShanghaiWiki/Wallop](http://bbs.wangjianshuo.com/ShanghaiWiki/Wallop)

> Wallop is Microsoft's answer to hot topics like blog, SNS, personal network... To fully leverage the five invitations, we will create a pool. The rule, as Run suggested, looks like this:
>
>
>
>
>
>
> -  Everyone need to accept this rule before requesting an invitation.
>
>
> -  In return, everyone should contribute *TWO* invitation back to the network.
>
>
> -  One will be dispatched as the wish of the person who granted the invitation, and
>
>
> -  the other one must send to others who registered on this page (the choice is yours)
>
>
>
>
>
> **Steps!!**
>
>
>
>
>
>
> -  Sign your name below the horizontal line
>
>
> -  Create an order before I distribute the first ticket
>
>
> -  When the list is ready, we will distribute the first ticket.
>
>
> -  The first one will send the invitation to the one below him/her. Meanwhile, anyone owes one invitation as the wish of the person whose name is immediately above him/her
>
>
> -  Now click **Edit Page** on the top of the page to get started.
>
