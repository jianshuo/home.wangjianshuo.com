The year of 2003 is passing by.

**Personal**

In this year, I got [married](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030317_marriage_announcement.htm), held the [big fat wedding ceremony](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030929_my_big_fat_wedding.htm). I didn't have any long trip (like [Daocheng](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/links/Travel/Daocheng/) of last year), but I paid short trips to nearby areas. It sounds crazy to cycle to [Taihu](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030622_back_from_taihu_by_bike.htm), [Chongming](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030612_chongming_trip.htm), [Tongli](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030702_tongli_beauty_at_night.htm), and [Pudong Airport](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030611_cycling_to_pudong_airport.htm). I also visited some unuasual places like [seashore](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031115_reached_sea_and_saw_faye_wang.htm), [30N 119E](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031215_incomplete_visit_to_30n_119e.htm). There are still some [Starbucks](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031122_starbucks_in_a_day.htm) stores I didn't visit this year yet. Maybe next year...

I [learnt to drive](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/links/Life/Learn/Driving/) and got my driver's license this year. I had good time to drive in this large city.

**Domestic**

This year is a bad year for China. SARS, fire, the taiwan's election and the blowout of natural gas... all claimed many people's life.

![](/archives/2003/12/28/screen-blow.out-news.jpg)

Image courtesy of Xinhua via Sina.com

**International**

The year of 2003 is also a bad year internationally. The Iraq war, mad cows, the Iran earthquake... bad news after bad news.

![](/archives/2003/12/28/screen-iran.earth.quake-news.jpg)

Image courtesy of MSNBC.com

2003 is a year of change.... [Isaac's post](http://www.isaacmao.com/2003/12/28/) confirmed that widely spread saying "2003 is a bad year". I hope the new start of 2004 will bring more good news to us.
