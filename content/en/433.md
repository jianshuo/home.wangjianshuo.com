With the new case of [SARS](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/links/News/SARS/) in Taiwan, the alert level of [Pudong Airport](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/links/Shanghai/Transportation/Pudong_Airport/) rised a little bit. I happen to go to the airport to pickup Wendy today. Passengers are asked to fillin the yellow health claim form before check-in and the broadcast repeatly (at a low frequency) to remind people to do so. Masks returned to some of the passengers' face of flights coming from Japan and Europe. It seems after the SARS period of this May, people are more experienced with the situation like this.
