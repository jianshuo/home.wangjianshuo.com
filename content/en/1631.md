I was in *Dukou Bookstore* today with [Hengge](http://Hengge.blogbus.com) and two other friends. After lunch at Hengge's home, and wandered near Blogbus's office, we finally decided to visit the newly opened bookstore. It is at the Julu Road, and the Fumin Road, in Jinan District.
