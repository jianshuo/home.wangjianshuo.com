Yesterday, I met my sister-in-law and other 5 teachers from the primary school I graduated. They just returned from Hangzhou and visited Shanghai. I took the assignment to arrange a cheap but comfortable hotel for them. It is not an easy job, since a hotel that is both cheap and good is not easy to find everywhere in the world.

I recommended Motel 168 which is near my previous [Vanke Waltz](http://www.wangjianshuo.com/personal/vanke/newhome/Garden/index.htm) House. I am glad that they are very happy with the small motel, and would like to recommend this hotel to my readers.

**Location**

*Motel 168* is a chain hotel that will have 10 more branches in Shanghai. The one they stayed last night is the Liuzhou Rd. Motel. It is relatively closer to [Xujiahui](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030811_shanghai_scenery_around_xujiahui.htm) and only 1,000 meters with the Metro Line #1.

![](/archives/2004/05/24/map-shanghai.liuzhou.rd-motel.jpg)

Map in courtesy of SMI

**Price**

Their standard room for two persons cost only **198 RMB** per night (or **25 USD**/night). That is very good price already. Their rooms are clean and the lobby/bars are very good. Here are some pictures.

**Lobby**

![](/archives/2004/05/24/shanghai.motel-lobby-v.jpg)

 © Jian Shuo Wang

The lobby looks better than some 3 stare hotels in China. The receiptions are very nice. Some of them speaks good English.

**Wash Room**

![](/archives/2004/05/24/shanghai.motel-wc.jpg)

 © Jian Shuo Wang

The wash room is clean without any bad smell.

**Room**

![](/archives/2004/05/24/shanghai.motel-room-entrance.jpg)

 © Jian Shuo Wang

They painted their rooms in vivid colors, like red, green and purple. This one is a green room.

![](/archives/2004/05/24/shanghai.motel-tv.jpg)

There is a TV in each room.

![](/archives/2004/05/24/shanghai.motel-sign-night.jpg)

 © Jian Shuo Wang. Sign outside the hotel

**Receiption**

![](/archives/2004/05/24/shanghai.motel-receiption.jpg)

 © Jian Shuo Wang.

Mobile charger. I remember if you put one RMB coin into the machine and it allow you to charge your mobile phone. They provided many kinds of outlet so one must match your mobile.

![](/archives/2004/05/24/shanghai.motel-mobile.charger.jpg)

**Entrance**

The entrance at night. As any other motel, it provides a large parking area.

![](/archives/2004/05/24/shanghai.motel-entrance-night.jpg)

The bar in the lobby of the motel. It is rare for such level (less than 25 USD) hotel to have a bar in Shanghai.

![](/archives/2004/05/24/shanghai.motel-bar-night.jpg)

 © Jian Shuo Wang

**Cards Accepted**

![](/archives/2004/05/24/shanghai.motel-cards.accepted.jpg)

 © Jian Shuo Wang

According to the plate, they accept Visa, MasterCard, Diners Club, American Express, JCB, and Yinliang - The China Bank Cards.

All rooms are covered by Wireless LAN.

**Detailed Map**

Want detailed map? I am leveraging my new Shanghai Map Viewer to located it.

It is near Metro Cao Bao Rd. Station.

**Booking**

The hotel is near the Everbright Exbition and Convention Center. The rooms are very hot and almost all occupied for most time. Very advanced booking is needed.

Their reservation hotline line is **+86-21-63168168**. Their website is [http://www.motel168.com/](http://www.motel168.com/).
