After the forecast of [Upcoming Change in Font Size](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040910_upcoming_change_in_font_size.htm), I uploaded the new [CSS](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/jianshuowangstyle.css) to my web server. I have formed a habbit to link to this CSS file whenever I create a new web page, so the most basic features of my style becomes handy. These features include:

- Verdana font

- Font size of 12pt (the default size in IE and most browsers). It was 10pt before.

- Colorful Headings.

- Margin on the left and on the right

Do you like the new design?

**More than Font Size**

Besides the change in font size, I made some minor changes to other parts for sake of usability improvement. This the second usability improvement on this site (first [Usability Improvement](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030514_usability_improvement.htm)). I conducted [Top Usability Mistakes of Wangjianshuo's Blog](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030506_top_usability_mistakes_of_wangjianshuos_blog.htm)) research one year and a quarter ago. I hope the site keep involving itself.

**Navigation Bar**

I changed the navigation bar a little bit so when you click Home and navigate to Home page, the Home does not has a link, implying you are on the page. The same applies to [Archives](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives.htm), [Directory](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/links), and [Map](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/scripts/map/). What is the usability guideline behind this?

> Visibility - By observing, users should know where they are and what is the possible actions they can perform
