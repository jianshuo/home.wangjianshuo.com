Tram is facing a very typical problem many visitors have. Because of the fast pace of chain hotels in China, most of the information is out of date, even on the website. Some are developing new branches at shock speed. When I blog about the [Motel 168](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040524_cheap_and_clean_motel_in_shanghai_motel168.htm), it just opened one branch near my home, and now, it is everywhere.

Let me help Tram out.

As of today (Febuary, 2008), Motel 168 has been spread out to many locations in Shanghai. Look at this map! This is the speed of expansion of a typical chain hotel in China. Besides the current 72 hotels, they have more to come.

So you see, the Gong He Xin Rd. hotel is marked as 28.
