I started the *[My City, My Bus Stop](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/wiki.php)* project last weekend. It is inspired by the [Degree Confluence Project](http://www.confluence.org) - a project aiming to visit and take picture of each of the latitude and longitude integer degree intersections in the world. I am thinking of a project to visit all the bus stops in Shanghai, take a picture and write simple description about it. The temporiy home page is hosted [here](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/wiki.php).

You may have noticed that I put a small icon

![](/archives/2004/01/07/screen-i.am.here-logo.PNG)

 on my home page. It links to the nearest bus stop of where I live.

I believe it is a great idea if many people (especially bloggers) join in to take pictures of the bus stop and add themselves to the database. They will form local communities around each bus stop...

The system is currently hosted on Wiki engine. It is a good way for online collaboration. Any one, yes, anyone who can see the page, can edit the page and add their own comment on the page. That is the power of [Wiki](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031205_wiki.htm).
