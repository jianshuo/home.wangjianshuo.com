If my friends or family came to Shanghai, I would recommend one of the three brand of chain hotels to them: [Motel 168](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040524_cheap_and_clean_motel_in_shanghai_motel168.htm), [Rujia Inns](http://www.homeinns.com/), and [Jin Jiang Inn](http://www.jj-inn.com/). They are cheap, clean and convinient.

Unlike those star hotels in Shanghai, their price is about 168 RMB - 298 RMB (20 USD - 36 USD) per night for the standard room. I took picture of one of the Motel168 room in this entry: [Cheap and Clean Motel in Shanghai - Motel168](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040524_cheap_and_clean_motel_in_shanghai_motel168.htm). The other two hostels are similiar. I cannot tell too much difference between these brands right now, since they are so similiar in price, room, and background.
