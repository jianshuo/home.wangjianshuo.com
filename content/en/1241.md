I have only been to Japan once.

If I hadn't tried to organize my old pictures, I even forget that I have been there for 5 hours at the end of 2004.

I enjoy travel, especially to places I have learnt about. For example, Japan.

Citizen in China can enter Japan without a visa for up to 72 hours. I have tried that. The only thing you need is a [shorepass](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20041209_getting_shore_pass_temp_visa_for_japan.htm), although I was [Rejected by ANA and Postponed Travel](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20041204_rejected_by_ana_and_postponed_travel.htm) once in Shanghai.

I planned for a long time for it. Here are the [pictures](http://www.flickr.com/photos/jianshuo) I found out in my picture folders on my laptop:
