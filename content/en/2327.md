The IFC (Hong Kong International Finance Center). Obviously it does not change over years, but every year, and the persons who take the photo should be the same as the [previous trip](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20070126_some_photo_of_hong_kong_in_jan_trip.htm), but every time, the feeling is still slightly different.

![](/archives/2008/12/06/DSC05591.JPG)

![](/archives/2008/12/06/DSC05592.JPG)

Hong Kong's ferry has a very lovely name: Star Ferry, or 天星小轮. It by itself is a highlight of my Hong Kong trip.

![](/archives/2008/12/06/DSC05608.JPG)

At night, the lights of buildings always shines under the clear sky:

![](/archives/2008/12/06/DSC05580.JPG)

The street scene of Hong Kong is pretty much similiar to Shanghai, reminding us of the deep connection between the two cities around 1940s.
