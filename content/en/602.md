[Eric](http://blog.joycode.com/mvm/) kindly lent me his [Sony Ericsson GC75 GPRS Wireless Modem](http://www.sonyericsson.com/gc75/).

![](/archives/2004/04/28/screen-sony.ericsson-picture.jpg)

Image in courtesy of [Sony Ericsson](http://www.sonyericsson.com/gc75/)

With this card and my GPRS enabled China Mobile SIM card, I can access Internet at any time. Here is the [driver](http://www.jbhg.com.cn/NOVATEL/sedownload/sonyeriPCcard.exe) (download from [jbhg.com.cn](http://www.jbhg.com.cn/download.asp)). It has pre-entered all the information needed to connect to China Mobile network.

After downloading, simply open the GC75 Manager and I am connected to the next work in few minutes. Now, I can update my blog from home. It is great!

![](/archives/2004/04/28/screen-gc75.manager.jpg)
