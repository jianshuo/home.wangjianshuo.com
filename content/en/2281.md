I am back from Beijing, via [train](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20081027_shanghai_to_beijing_train_is_better_than_air_.htm). Here is a short summary.

**Train is Good**

As I [described](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20081027_shanghai_to_beijing_train_is_better_than_air_.htm) earlier, train is efficient for business travel. But I realized there is one criteria for it to be efficient: you have to be able to sleep very well on the train. Like me, I am always [sleepy](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030118_me_a_sleepy_cat.htm), and I can sleep from the time I get to bed on the train until train is approaching the terninal station.  For my friend, Jia, the trip is not that pleasant - he wakes up every one hour or sooner during the trip.
