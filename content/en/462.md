There is not much change in my life recently. The weather is still cold and became colder these days. It seems the cold weather has frozen my life too. I no longer think about going out and won't do crazy things like [cycling to Taihu](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030622_back_from_taihu_by_bike.htm) or [visiting a degree confluence point](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031215_incomplete_visit_to_30n_119e.htm). The city life is the same - wake up, rush to crowded metro, step into office and sit down. The world is as small as a computer screen. The connection to the out side world are two lines - one network cable and one telephone line. Friends become smiley faces

![](/archives/2003/08/12/09/23.gif)

 in my MSN Messenger. A dinner at a hot pot restaurant is not expensive and can always please two of us. :-) Life at the age of 27 is not as exciting as that in younger ages. -- I still remember a dinner at KFC needed two weeks' planning when I was in university. Now KFC has became the last place I will go for dinner. That is progress. That is also the lose of fun.
