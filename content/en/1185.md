**P.S. Previous Issue of BusinessWeek on Microsoft**

The Sept 26, 2005 issue of [BusinessWeek](http://www.BusinessWeek.com) is wonderful. I read almost every single article on this issue. The article "[Troubling Exits At Microsoft](http://www.businessweek.com/magazine/content/05_39/b3952001.htm)" is a good one, as other articles in the magazine. I was surprised to two things: 1) BusinessWeek is sensitive to new stuff like AJAX, Tag, or Cyworld. 2) One statement in comment on this site is true: "No readers are interested in the questions interviewees are willing to answer". There are many tough questions for Steve Baller. One example was "[some employees] question whether you should be CEO. What's your response?". OMG...
