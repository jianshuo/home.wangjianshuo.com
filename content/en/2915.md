The top secret I have about this city is how to [Get to Top of SWFC Within 100 RMB](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20091003_get_to_top_of_swfc_within_100_rmb.htm). Now, I found out that trick does not work.

The 91th floor of Shanghai World Financial Center is no longer the best value bar in Shanghai. Now, they require 120 RMB per person minimum pay for all afternoon tea guests.

Now, the only alternatives left for me are:

- [Bottom of the Well at Jinmao Tower](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20070101_the_great_well_in_jinmao_tower.htm). They require 50 RMB / person fee. It is fine, since their juice and tea are 50 RMB.

- Lobby of the [JW Marriott](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031028_jw_marriott_in_tomorrow_square_opens.htm) in Puxi
