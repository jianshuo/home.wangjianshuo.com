Today is the one year anniversary for Wangjianshuo's Blog. The website was setup on Sept 11, 2002, which is exactly one year ago.

**The Begining**

At that time, I was inspired by a blog [RussianLegs.com](HTTP://Russianlegs.com) on [Sept 3, 2003](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030113_blogger_in_china.htm), I am thinking about setting up my own blog.

So on Sept 11, 2003 (just coincident to the one year anniversary of Sept 11 event), I setup MovableType on the desktop computer in my reading room (the computer has been selt out at 2500 RMB later), and wrote my first blog entry [MovableType Successfully Installed on Windows XP](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20020911_movabletype_successfully_installed_on_windows_xp.htm). It turned out to one of the most valuable entry on the whole website. I am happy that this entry helped a lot of bloggers to start MovableType on Windows based platform.

**The Effort**

From that day, I started to write daily on this blog. This is what Geo from Russianlegs.com told me when I asked how much time he used to maintain his blog: "Everyday, I spent 15 minutes to write the blog and 15 minutes to edit it".

I followed the rule but some time I spent more than 1 hour on a long article and sometimes only take 5 minutes to note down what is happening.

**The Blog**

Now one year has past, here is the statistic for the blog (as you can see on the right side of my home page)

Created: Sept 11, 2002

Entries: 316

Categories: 7

Page views/day: ~ 4K

Comments: 2387

Location: Shanghai, China

Author: Jian Shuo Wang

Update Frequency: daily

It seems I am doing pretty well in the last year that I created 316 entries in the 365 days, making write rate of 87%. This certain include the long week in Daocheng when there is no mobile signal, no electricity and let alone network. The second long absence in blogging is in [February](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030223_update_about_these_weeks.htm), when I was on busy business trip to Beijing. Besides that, I believe I ALMOS kept this blog updated everyday. Wow. What an accomplishment, I didn't think about that I can keep doing something continuous for one year or longer. I typically would give up half way or earlier.

**The Reason**

The reasons I kept the hard work (sometimes) are the feedbacks and comments on this blog. It is such an interesting place that people share the same interest gather and interactive with other. I got 2387 comments under 316 posts, making the average comment/post rate 7.55. That is impressive number, I believe. From the comments, I see people are happy either because I provided some valuable information about topics like [Shanghai](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/categories/shanghai.htm), [SARS](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/categories/sars.htm), [Pudong Airport](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/categories/pvg.htm), [OT715](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030311_alcatel_ot715.htm) or Windows Media web cam, or because I shared my personal life and experience like going to [Daocheng](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/categories/daocheng.htm), [learn to drive](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030628_learn_to_drive.htm), even to [buy a mountain](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030828_what_can_i_do_with_my_own_mountain.htm). Actually, I was really flattered by the good words.

**The Popularity**

In the previous year, the site becomes a little bit famous among search engines and media. My Google [PageRank reached PR5](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20021128_my_google_pagerank_increases_to_pr5.htm) and become the target for some common term, like [Pu Dong Airport](http://www.google.com/search?sourceid=navclient&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&q=Pu+Dong+Airport). Meanwhile, it was featured on some local magazines like [Shanghai Magazine](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030416_wangjianshuocom_featured_in_shanghai_magzine.htm). Thus the highest hit rate reached to 5K some time.

**The Friends**

I also treasure the relationship I built within the blogging world. I have a lot of friends who monitors and reference to my site consistently but I never linked back. It is because they are all in Chinese and from my site log, majority of my readers cannot read Chinese. I felt bad not doing that. These sites are: [Chinese Tea](http://christina.bizbeez.com) (by Christina), [IsaacMao.com](http://isaacmao.com) (by Isaac Mao), [Joycode](http://blog.joycode.com) (by Joy Wang), [Tiger Cafe](http://rg.blogspot.com) (by Raymond) and lots of [others sites](http://www.google.com/search?hl=en&lr=&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&q=link%3Ahome.wangjianshuo.com).

I'd like to celebrate the one year anniversary and share the happiness with you. I'd like to thank Wendy for her support. Click [here](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives.htm)for a complete list of the 316 articles.
