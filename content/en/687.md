Yahoo! China launched [Yisou.com](http://www.yisou.com), the Yahoo! search engine in China. I am a keen fan of Google and I love everything from Google - the search engine, the GMail, the Adsense program, and funny things in their lab. I thought Google was the best in search engine world. However, I start to doubt that. Yisou.com seems to be doing a better job in the Chinese market. It is so similar with Google.

Yisou seems to have indexed more content than Google. For example, if you search [Wangjianshuo](http://www.google.com/search?sourceid=navclient&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&q=wangjianshuo) in Google, you get **5,150** results (as of today). If you search [Wangjianshuo](http://yisou.com/search?p=wangjianshuo) in Yisou, **7,640** items were returned. Also, the speed of [Yisou](http://www.yisou.com) is really faster than Google - it is almost the same speed as Google when Google just started. It may because not many people use Yisou now.

**Google invests into Baidu**

It is also reported that [Google](http://www.google.com) invested in [Baidu](http://www.baidu.com), the other top search engine in China. It is interesting that Baidu got the opportunity to grow up during the time when Google was banned.

**Yipai**

Another auction website, [yipai.com](yipai.com), was launched recent. It is a joint effort of [Yahoo! China](http://cn.yahoo.com) and [Sina](http://www.sina.com.cn). I believe it will gain market share rapidly from Eachnet. The functions and interfaces are much better than Eachnet.
