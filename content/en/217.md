**Tibet said "no" to travellers**

Recently, the National Tourism Administration has forbidden any foreign tourist to enter Tibet. Later, domestic tourism groups are forbidden to enter Tibet too due to the spreading of [SARS](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/categories/sars.htm). I don't know when it will resume so we can go to the [dream-like Tibet](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030305_dreamlike_tibet.htm) again.

**East China Normal Universtity closed its door for the whole May**

According to [cotton](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030502_with_sars_all_we_have_is_time.htm#0953):

> ECNU made a rule that all the students (including shanghai students) should stay in school the whole May. No one can go home.

Universities in Shanghai have took a lot of actions to protect their students from the risk to get infected by SARS.
