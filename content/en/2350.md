NCUSCR

Besides my daily work, I am honored to be involved in many NCUSCR (National Committee of US China Relationships) program. I was invited by Haisong to join the [lunch with delegatem](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20060629_ncucr_visit.htm) from [Public Intellectuals Program](http://ncuscr.org/PIP/PublicIntellectualsProgram.htm). From then, I started my journey to directly work on something related to US-China relationship, and later joined YLF (Young Leaders Forum), the best organization I have ever been so far, and later, hosted some delegation of US Mayor groups, and Congress group.

They helped me to open my eyes to see a much larger group of people than my daily life and my daily work can possible bring to me, and through the programs, I got many great friends.
