The topic of this year's Young Leader Forum is: **Challenge**.

After I got the exciting [invitation](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20070830_got_invitation_of_young_leaders_forum.htm) for [Young Leaders Forum](http://www.ncuscr.org/Young_Leaders_Forum/Young_Leaders_Forum.htm) of [National Committee on United States-China Relationship](http://ncuscr.org). [Haisong](http://www.businessweek.com/bwdaily/dnflash/apr2000/nf00411a.htm) was a member; [Bo Shao](http://shaoblog.com) is a member; so was [Robin Li](http://ir.baidu.com/phoenix.zhtml?c=188488&p=irol-govBio&ID=138201). I am looking forward to the great meeting in Nanjing at the end of November this year.

So far, to prepare to the little presentation with the theme Challenge, I am thinking of this topic:

> Choices in a World of Different Rules

I am still thinking about how to structure this presentation.

At the same time, I want to ask, if you are going to do a presentation of 15 minutes about the topic: challenge, what would you say?

P.S. The YLF will start from Nov 28, 2007 to Dec 2, 2007, with an extended trip in Shanghai. I don't know where the next year's U.S trip will be, but it should also be great..
