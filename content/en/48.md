"Everyday objects tell interesting stories...." By describing the objects coming into my house, "I'm preserving memories of my time here" - as Scott did in [Leaving Berlin](http://www.spies.com/~scott/berlin/content/01.11.15.htm).

Yesterday, I bought three [IKEA Billy Bookcases](http://www.ikea-usa.com/product_presentation/syscat.asp?id=410,6012) and a Bialitt double bed.

![](/archives/2002/10/21/243879475_db458e8fdd_t.jpg)

*Image source: IKEA web site*

I have one IKEA Billy bookcase

![](/personal/meilong/small/shanghai.meilong.house-Billy.bookcase_small.jpg)

 already. I bought that one in July, 1999 using my first month salary. It was twice the current price before Billy became on sale this year. I have to say Billy is very well designed and useful. The interesting thing is, I loved the light color three years ago and was quite sure the light wood color was the best among all the colors Billy series offered. But now, I love the dark one better, so I made the painful decision to switch to the new color series and bought three medium brown Billy bookcase - one 80x202cm, two 60x202cm and one height extension unit.

It took me two nights to assembly the bookcases and the bed - especially the Bialitt bed. It is the most complicated furniture in IKEA I have ever bought.
