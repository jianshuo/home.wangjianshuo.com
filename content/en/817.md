I'd like to share the Orange Harvest in my [garden](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040606_mowing_lawn.htm). I didn't expect so many fruit for the first year of this orange tree. The orange is very sweet right now. It seems there is not much different from the orange in supermarket, expect it is a little bit smaller and are completely green.
