**More Companies are Hiring**

Microsoft of cause started aggressive hiring. I know the development center have more than 200 open headcounts. Check this [page](http://members.microsoft.com/careers/international/default.asp?lang=EN&loc=CHN). Many job opening there, even not included the hiring plan.

Google is hiring. The effort led by [Kai-Fu Lee](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20050721_kaifu_joined_google_china.htm). The typically Google white-background poster already appeared on university campus.

Yahoo! announced very agressive hiring plan also - it was said 400?
