[Edward](http://www.nikonfans.org/) is my colleague a [MCS](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040706_transferred_to_microsoft_consulting_service.htm). He is a serious photographer with powerful digital canon and extreme passion on equipments and taking pictures.

I read his blog today and found some very interesting post. He is also in Shanghai and he sits near my cubicle. I guess the city from his eyes is also attractive to people who are moving to Shanghai.

At mid-Sept, he used the camera on his SmartPhone to catch the [scene](http://www.nikonfans.org/archives/life/000072_chasing_thief.html) of a chief caught by police man. It bring my camera with me most of the time but I always feel I should bring it more frequently when I see something suddenly happen.

His photo of [Cocacola](http://www.nikonfans.org/archives/travel/000018_mgb_closing_and_cocacola_macys_mall.html) and [CNN](http://www.nikonfans.org/archives/travel/000013_cnn_git_visiting.html) very impressive. It convinced me that a better camera is still important to a high quality photo.

The best part is [the F1 photos](http://www.nikonfans.org/archives/life/000074_gas_monster_f1_shanghai.html). Thanks to his longer lens camera so he can caught more details of the racing cars than I [did](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040926_f1_grand_prix_shanghai_pictures.htm).

There are many other interesting stuff on that site. Part of it was written in English and the other in Chinese. Enjoy the nice blog.
