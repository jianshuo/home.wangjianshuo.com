What happens these days. After the [New York Times article about this little blog](http://www.nytimes.com/2010/02/25/technology/25iht-rshanblog.html), Forbes named this little me as [Nine Young Chinese Entrepreneurs To Watch](http://www.forbes.com/2010/02/26/young-chinese-entrepreneurs-to-watch-entrepreneurs-technology-china_2.html) ([picture edition](http://www.forbes.com/2010/02/26/young-chinese-entrepreneurs-to-watch-entrepreneurs-technology-china_slide_10.html)). It is a little bit surprising and embarrassing, since when [Hannah Seligson](http://www.hannahseligson.com/) emailed back and forth about the description, I didn't know it was under this title. The title in my brain is "Lifestyle of Chinese young man"...

Anyway, thanks Hannah, and Forbes for the article. It did generate some congratulations emails in my inbox, but I just want to put a disclaimer here for the record: it is not a real ranking, just some "sample" of entrepreneurs, and I happened to have mutual friends with the kind author.
