I met with [Weigend](http://www.flickr.com/photos/aweigend/8505347/), the former Chief Scientist of Amazon.com in Shanghai tonight. It was a great experience to talk with them on the new ideas around blogging and social software. I totally agree his two principles on new generation of software: 1) Fast Feedback 2) Low threshold for participation. Dr. Weigend believes with tags and analysing of user's behavior, we can create something very new. I agree. He took [a picture of us](http://www.flickr.com/photos/aweigend/8505347/) with his Palm and send it to Flickr. :-)
