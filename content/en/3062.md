It is official, and it is, as always, last minute, that I am going to host a small Wangjianshuo's Blog Meetup in New York on Saturday (Oct 8, 2011), which is tomorrow, at 5:30 PM at the following location:

> Starbucks
>
> 665 Broadway (cnr. E 2nd Street)
>
> New York, NY 10012

It will last for one hour until 6:30 pm.

**About the Meetup**

This blog has been running for 9 years, and we have meetup from time to time. The first was in [2003 in Shanghai](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031227_wonderful_meetup_on_dec_26.htm), and few times later (like [this one in 2008](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20080830_the_wonderful_2008_meetup.htm)). It also happened in other places in the world, like [San Francisco](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20050424_meetup_in_san_francisco_ca.htm), and [Palo Alto](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20070908_meetup_in_palo_alto_on_sept_9_2007.htm). This is the first time it comes to New York. But as always, as a person who is incapable of planning anything in advance, it is always in the last-minute announcement style.

If you are planning to come, please quickly post a comment either via Facebook, or using the old comment function (I am still thinking about how to integrate them together). It is OK if you show up without comment - it is in extremely informal way.
