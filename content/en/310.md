Today, I found very interesting stuff - the [Degree Confluence Project](http://www.confluence.org) via this [thread](http://digiblog.org/forum/viewforum.php?forum=4&55) (Chinese) of [Digiblog.org](http://digiblog.org)

> The goal of the project is to visit each of the latitude and longitude integer degree intersections in the world, and to take pictures at each location. The pictures and stories will then be posted here
>
> Credit: [Confluence.org](http://www.confluence.org/)

What an interesting project it. It reminded us there is only one earth. This is a very good reference for students to learn geography - I hope I had a teacher who knew about this project when I was at school. The pictures and the practice greatly help to learn the planet earth.

**My Plan to Reach 40°N 116°E #1**

There are 965 confluence points in China. 68 points were visited, making 7% of all the points. There is only one point in Shanghai at [31°N 121°E](http://www.confluence.org/region.php?id=563). The point was visited by [Frank Yu](http://www.confluence.org/visitor.php?id=2537) and [Sam Chang](http://www.confluence.org/visitor.php?id=2538) at the end of the year before last year. It seems relatively simple.

I am drafting my plan to visit the only point in Beijing at 40°N 116°E

![](http://www.confluence.org/mapgen.php?region=543)

Image courtesy of [Confluence.org](http://www.confluence.org/region.php?id=543)

[Keith Ketterer](http://www.confluence.org/visitor.php?id=1834) and his family has attempted to go there but was not successful due to the unfavorable location of the point. Maybe I can have the second attempt to go there. It is near the downtown Beijing. I am planning to go there by bike so the trip is more flexible. This plan may be completed by the end of this year. The point may need some climbing work and may need ropes.
