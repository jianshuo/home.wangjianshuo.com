It is moving season. Joi Ito is also [moving his blog](http://joi.ito.com/archives/2005/12/21/moving.html) to a new machine - according to Jio, he was changing IP address. DNS always take long time to replicate. I hosted my DNS with [www.net.cn](http://www.net.cn). I hope it will be working in the years to come.

Yesterday, talked with Yale in the decent place called Peace Masion (Wendy [mentioned](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20050907_we_filled_our_lives_but_lost_our_souls.htm) this place in her blog). We talked about lots of "crazy business" models, like the [million dollar homepage](http://milliondollarhomepage.com/). (In China, it was translated as Bai Wan Ge Zi - million boxes. I believe it is a great translation to reflect the pixel nature of the "business).

These days, Joi Ito also [discussed](http://joi.ito.com/archives/2005/12/20/eweek_typosquatters_are_gaming_google.html) about some *strange* business, like [typo-domains](http://cyber.law.harvard.edu/people/edelman/typo-domains/). I believe to really survive in the Internet world, people need to be sensitive enough to these "trends" and "tricks".

Yesteday, chatted with [Xdanger](http://www.xdanger.com/) with another site: [9991.com](http://9991.com). Its [rank](http://www.alexa.com/data/details/traffic_details?q=&url=9991.com) raised incredibably high. Within two months, it is the No. 596 largest website in term of traffic in the world. There are lot of untold story behind it though.

On Xdanger's blog, he [mentioned](http://www.xdanger.com/2005/11/17/000029.html) the other [700 replica](http://www.pixelsitelinks.com/other-pixel-sites-links.html) of [milliondollarhomepage](http://milliondollarhomepage.com). I was surprised how many people replicating the same idea. I actually should not be surprised, since setting up a website or selling online, or blogging is an idea that many people practice everyday.

Copying a successful business model *seems* to be a shortcut to money (not neccessarily success). Last time, people juked that there are C2C model (Copy to China) and B2C model (Bring to China). It was funny.

**It is cold**

Yesterday was [Dongzhi](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031222_dongzhi_winter_solstice.htm), or winter solstice. It is the shortest day for locations in the north part of the earth.

According to traditional Chinese saying, after this day, we count days in nines. Every nine day will be counted as a "*jiu*". After three *jiu*, the temperature will drop to the coldest according to how people feel. That is about 27 days from today.

**Got Cold**

I got gold cold these days due to the exteremely cold weather, and the recent backup/restore/recover work was also anonying. When something happens to digital files like website, people will always regret that we don't have the latest backup.

Hours after it happened, I drove out to computer store to buy DVD Writer, so I can backup every file or picture I have to my DVD. I drove very carefully, and sometimes slow. Wendy asked why, I said: "None of us have backups for ourselves...."
