We went to the [Shanghai Circus World](http://www.circus-world.com/) today for the circus show. It is the first time I go there. It was wonderful experience for me. I'd like to suggest everyone to go there to see the acrobats, the animals, and the motos.... The good thing is, they allow cameras and photographing during the show, so I captured some pictures during the 2-hour performance.

Many of the shows are breath-taking.

**Where is the Circus**

You can reach there by Metro. It is at the Shanghai Circus station of Metro Line #1 (north extension). If you drive, go along the Chengdu Elevated Road, heading north. After passing the Inner Ring, leave the elevated highway at Yan Chang Road 延长路 and take U-turn at Guangzhong Road 广中路. Their telephone is +86-21-66527750, +86-21-66522395.
