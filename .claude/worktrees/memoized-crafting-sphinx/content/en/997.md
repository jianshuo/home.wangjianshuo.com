March 15 is the day I **[brought my car Goudaner](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040315_jian_shuo_got_his_car.htm) home one year ago. The blog entry of that day reminded me that I put 40.2L of gas (not oil, as pointed by readers) into my car. How excited I was!  didn't get used to the feeling that my own car is downstairs waiting for me that night. It was a strange feeling.

In the past 365 days, Goudaner changed my life a lot. This sweet partner also experienced a lot. It was [scratched by drunk driver](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040915_goudaner_scratched_by_drunk_driver.htm). Fortunately, it is the most serious accident we had. The car price [dropped](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040322_big_drop_in_price_of_my_car.htm) by 5000 RMB the second day I bought, and continued to drop to around 80K RMB after that - a 20% decrease. Meanwhile the gas price [went up](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040401_gasoline_price_goes_up.htm). The model [FIAT Siena 1.5 HL](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040316_fiat_siena_15_hl_pictures.htm) is a nice one, and I enjoy the mannual shift very well, but Wendy obviously don't like to too much. She changed her display name to "[I Hate Nanpu Bridge](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/fanfan/20050314_585.htm)" recently, because during rush hours, she was packed on the 3 kms of continous upslope. That is not easy for new drivers.

We have named the day Goudaner coming to our home as its birthday. Nina [suggested](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040316_fiat_siena_15_hl_pictures.htm#0216) me to give a name to the new car:

> I don't know whether there is enough of a car-owning culture yet in China to have developed the custom of pet names for cars. I think I've read that about 25% of Americans have named their cars and supposedly, cars that have names last longer and are more reliable, although presumably this is because the sort of people who name their cars usually take good care of them, rather than by magic.

It is a very nice advice. A name for the car gives me the feeling that it has life and feelings. It may feel the pain if we switch the shift unproperly or someone hurt it. It is no longer steel and rubber for me. It is just like a pet and part of my life :-)

![](/archives/2004/03/16/shanghai-fiat.side.jpg)

Goudaner, one year ago. [src](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040316_fiat_siena_15_hl_pictures.htm)

Happy birthday, Goudaner. You are one year old now and you brings a lot to Wendy and me.
