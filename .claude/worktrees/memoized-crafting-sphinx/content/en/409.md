Today, I continued my walk tour to the rest of the Starbucks. Due to the limitation of time and being unfamiliar with Pudong Area, I only visited 4 of all the 5 Stores in Pudong. They are:

[Bin Jiang Store](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031122_starbucks_in_a_day.htm#binjiang)

[Super Brand Site Store](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031122_starbucks_in_a_day.htm#superbrand)

[Mori Tower Store](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031122_starbucks_in_a_day.htm#moritower)

[Times Square Store](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031122_starbucks_in_a_day.htm#timessquare)

The pictures and descriptions are consolidated to [yersterday's page](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031122_starbucks_in_a_day.htm).

It was really cold in Pudong and there are not many people. There are barely no customers in Mori Tower Store and Times Square Store. In contract, Bin Jiang Store is always crowded - it is said it has the best view in all Starbucks around the world. Is it true?
