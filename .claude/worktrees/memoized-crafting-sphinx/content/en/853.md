I saw the two cats in my garden again. Poor little pets! The [winter](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20041101_winter_in_shanghai.htm) came and they don't have a place to stay. They were very cold in the morning wind. I guess they are abandoned since I frequently saw them in my garden. Wendy took [picture](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/fanfan/20041012_319.htm) ([I](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/fanfan/images/cat.jpg/cat.JPG), [II](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/fanfan/images/cat1.jpg/cat1.JPG)) of them last month, when it was pretty warm.

![](/archives/2004/11/03/shanghai-two.cats-staring.jpg)

© Jian Shuo Wang

Above: The baby cat hide under its month's body to keep warm.

![](/archives/2004/11/03/shanghai-mother.cat.jpg)

© Jian Shuo Wang

![](/archives/2004/11/03/shanghai-baby.cat.jpg)

© Jian Shuo Wang

Above: I gave them some cookie and they enjoyed it very much.

Later, I setup a temp home for them with a big box. After I arrived home, I found the two little cats have settled down in the paper box. It is by any means better than nothing. The boxes protected them from cold winter wind.
