This is the forth month memorial day after Wendy and I got married. It is always sweet to look back to the long road we have come along.

I happen to see the picture in [Dan Washburn](http://www.danwashburn.com)'s article - *Build Me Up, Tear Me Down* on [That's Shanghai](http://www.thatsmagazines.com) Magazine - **Growing Old Together gracefully.**

I feel warm when I read it. Seems to God has intentionally show the sentence to me on my special day. :-)

Recently, my friend and my favorite blogger Christina in Nanjing [got married](http://christina.bizbeez.com/index.php?p=1671&c=1#comments). I'd like to congratulate her and David for their wedding and best wishes to them. Christina was nervous at the time - I can understand that but I am sure the decision is the one that mark the beginning of happen times, like mine. :-)
