When I started my website one year ago, I only have [www.wangjianshuo.com](http://www.wangjianshuo.com) and some very simple page. I don't remember the PR value of that site since I didn't know about this magic word.

Later, I found it is ranked PR4. I love this number very much since most of the sites are PR3 or PR4. Some of them are very large sites.

These days, I found the PR for both of my [Personal site](http://www.wangjianshuo.com) and [blog site](http://home.wangjianshuo.com) reach PR5.

**This is very encouraging news**

Why? Since I get listed higher in the result of Google search engine. Sometimes I don't know why, but my sites are among the top 10 for certain keyword. Sometimes, it even appears as No. 1 site.

**Interesting results**

"*[Net send spam](http://www.google.com/search?q=net+send+spam&hl=en&lr=&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&safe=off&output=search)*" - it returns my blog entry of ["Net Send" - Yet Another Type of Spam](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20020923_net_send_yet_another_type_of_spam.htm) as the top result record - sometimes as the second one.

"*[Pu Dong Airport](http://www.google.com/search?hl=en&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&q=pu+dong+airport)*" - the first three entries comes from my site.

"*[Hai Nan](http://www.google.com/search?hl=en&lr=&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&safe=off&q=hai+nan)*" - also brings a lot of visitors.

It is interesting.

**Why this will happen**

I believe it is due to two factors:

- My friends are linking to me. [Raymond](http://rg.blogspot.com), [Christina](http://christina.bizbeez.com), [cnblog.org](http://cnblog.org) and [Patrick](http://cwang.blogspot.com)'s site helped to increase my PageRank. Thanks. I will link back in a special section.

- Two sites interlinking each other. I didn't realized it before but I found [home.wangjianshuo.com](http://home.wangjianshuo.com) provides 80+ links to [www.wangjianshuo.com](http://www.wangjianshuo.com) and receives 100+ links from it. This may also cause the upgrade of PageRank for both sites.

**More on PageRank**

[Google's got a secret all want to know](http://www.globeandmail.com/servlet/ArticleNews/PEstory/TGAM/20021121/TWGOOG/Science/science/science_temp/1/1/2/)

**Updated** Find out PageRank of your site *December 29, 2003*

The easiest way is to download [Google Toolbar](http://toolbar.google.com/). After you install the toolbar, visit your website and your PageRank will be displayed as a small green bar on the toolbar. Move your mouse over the small green bar (marked as PageRank) and the numeric value (0-10) will be displayed as tool tip.
