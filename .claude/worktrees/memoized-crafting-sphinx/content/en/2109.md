After [boycotting Carrefour](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20080411_friends_started_to_boycott_french_products.htm), this is the scene I took in Carrefour on May 3, 2008.

**There are Less People, but Much Lesser than I Expected**

This is the main "street" in the shopping mall.  It is rarely like this! It is almost empty in Shanghai standard. It is daily scene in many shopping malls in US, but in Shanghai, it is called **empty**.

![](/archives/2008/CarrefouronMay3_10D50/image.png)

This area is almost empty, with no customers. In the cooked food area, I counted, and found out the number of customers are less than the staff behind the food counter.

![](/archives/2008/CarrefouronMay3_10D50/image_7.png)

![](/archives/2008/CarrefouronMay3_10D50/image_9.png)

This is the checkout area. No lines at all.

![](/archives/2008/CarrefouronMay3_10D50/image_8.png)

Out of the 30 or so counters, almost half of them are closed with signs like this:

 

![](/archives/2008/CarrefouronMay3_10D50/image_10.png)

For the open counters, there are no lines.

![](/archives/2008/CarrefouronMay3_10D50/image_11.png)

This is the scene of the checkout counters - again, not many customers.

![](/archives/2008/CarrefouronMay3_10D50/image_13.png)

Well. What do you think?

**Facts about the Photos**

- The photos are taken in the Xinlicheng Stone of Carrefour, at the corner of South Yanggao Road, and Gaoqing Road.
- It was around 1:00 PM today on Saturday - typically the time has fewer customers than peak hours. The third day of May holiday may also contribute to the lack of customers.
- As any photo with intention, I took the photo to show how few people I saw, so I may wait for 10 or 20 seconds to take a photo with fewer people than random shot. Hopefully this is not too misleading. The point is, in normal days, it is even hard to take a photo similar to this scene.

In short, I want my readers to know that there are some errors in what is reflected in these photos from the facts. It does not seem THAT bad, although everyone should be safe to conclude that the business of this store is seriously impacted.
