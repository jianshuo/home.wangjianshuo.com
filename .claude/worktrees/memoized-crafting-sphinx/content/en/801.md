Almost half year after I [moved to Pudong](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040405_life_in_pudong_started.htm), I finally get my home in good order. Today, as Wendy and I celebrate the 1.5 year wedding anniversary, we welcame our first batch of guests: [Grace](http://blog.joycode.com/grace), [Claire](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/claire), [Xiao Gao](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/gao), and Ricky. They share something in common - all of them are best friends and all of them keep blogs. It is late after the party and I will write more about the new home soon. The reason why it takes so long to settle down is, we have more than 60 boxes and we have to clean them one by one and put the stuff in the boxes into their new place in the new home. So from today, I guess the life is completely back to normal. It seems to long a process, isn't it?
