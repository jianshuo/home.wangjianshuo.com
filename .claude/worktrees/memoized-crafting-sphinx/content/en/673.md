What an intersting and crazy idea: [ATMs as WiFi hotspots](http://www.jacobsen.no/anders/blog/). It is a cool idea because

- It has electricity power

- It must have a telephone line

- It is expensive (so people don't care too much to put a Wifi AP)

- It is placed in crowded business centers where people more likely need WIFI

- People can easily pay for WIFI (if they are not free).

The original idea comes from [nyc at typepad](http://nyc.typepad.com/e/2004/06/atmwifi_hotspot.html) but I cannot access the site via my dial up to **16300** (User name 16300, password 16300). It seems [typepad.com](http://typepad.com) has been blocked. Anyway, the idea is cool. I love it.

How I eager to find a WIFI very soon? I am getting back from [WIFI](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030517_my_home_goes_wireless.htm) to dial up. There is no [ADSL](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040604_adsl_is_also_unavailable_in_my_home.htm), no [Cable Modem](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040603_cable_modem_unavaible_in_pudong.htm) in Pudong. I just found out the FTTB+LAN by Telecom is also not opened yet although the line has been deployed to my room. I miss the [Network Infrastructure](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20020913_network_infrustucture_in_my_home_in_shanghai.htm) in my previous home in Pudong. As you can see, the above are major broadband solutions in Shanghai: ADSL, Cable Modem, China Telecom FTTB+LAN, Greatwall Broadband...
