I saw requests to me to remove some comments people made on this blog. I suggest everyone to check out my [Emails and Privacy Policies](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030508_emails_and_privacy_policies.htm), especially the section on [censorship](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030508_emails_and_privacy_policies.htm#censorship).

To put it simple, my [principle on comments](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030403_thanks_for_your_comments.htm#principle) is not do delete any comment as long as it is legal, not spam and is readable, and (this one is important) it is NOT personal attack.

I don't like flaming comments that judges someone else's personality, generalize them to people from a country, with certain religions belief, or simply the language they use. I do remove these comments.

Just be back from the trip, and it is about 10 days' comments I didn't reply. Let me take some time to clear these backlogs.

Again, thanks for commenting.
