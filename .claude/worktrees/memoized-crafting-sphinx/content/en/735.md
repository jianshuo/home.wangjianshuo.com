Blogcn, the largest blogging service provider, shut down their server again. This time, it seems because of technical difficulties.

![](/archives/2004/07/27/screen-blogcn-down.again.jpg)

Image in courtesy of [blogcn.com](http://www.blogcn.com)

As far as I know, there close friends of mine has been affected. Jackgu commented in his last post that he has lost passion for blogging since the 10-day downtime broke his pace of updating frequency and leaving unbearable blank on the blog history.

Claire and Eric were also driven mad. I am proud to help them to host their blogs on wangjianshuo.com. Their new homes are:

- Claire: [http://home.wangjianshuo.com/claire](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/claire)

- Eric: [http://home.wangjianshuo.com/mvm](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/mvm)

I love their writing styles so much. These two blogs, along with Wendy's [blog](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/fanfan) are the three best personal blogs I saw (in my friend circle). I am happy that I won't lose them again.

Meanwhile, I still encourage people to start host their blogs by themselves as soon as possible. Check this entry: [Blog, to Host it or Not?](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040721_blog_to_host_it_or_not.htm) for reasons.

I hope Eric and Claire enjoy the new home (not fancy but stable home). I hope one day, they can move to their permanent address at [http://www.clairehu.com](http://www.clairehu.com) and [http://www.zhengziying.com](http://www.zhengziying.com), where their blog should reside at.
