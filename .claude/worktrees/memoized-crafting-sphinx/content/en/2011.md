This is the snow scene of my garden again, taken in yesterday morning.

![](/archives/2008/SnowSceneinShanghaiAgain_143BD/DSC03294.jpg)

Compare the photo above to this one. The below one was taken on Feb 1, 2005, in my article [Heavy Snow in Shanghai](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20050101_heavy_snow_in_shanghai.htm). I should not have called it heavy snow compared to this year's snow. Of cause, everything is relative. It is nothing for many places, like Canada, but it is really rare in Shanghai.

![](/archives/2005/01/01/shanghai-snow-outside.jpg)

>
>
>
> Hey, Hey, Shanghai is snowing...
>
>
>
> Incrediable! Wowo. It is snowing in Shanghai!

I even explained to many friends that [there IS snow](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20050222_yes_it_snowed_in_shanghai.htm) in winter in Shanghai, although it snowed just once every two or three years.
