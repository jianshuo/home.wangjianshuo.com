"Dave Sifry, founder of Technorati, told me once that getting a link is like receiving a gift. It's a social gesture." (via [Scobleizer](http://radio.weblogs.com/0001011/2004/06/01.html)). What a fantastic idea. I pays attention to my referral log to all incoming links - just curious abut what others say about me or my blog. It should be the same for most bloggers. Gift is a sweet word, just like the feeling of getting a link. Let me distribute some gift today.

**Isaac**

Isaac is a smart guy. His personal website [isaacmao.com](http://www.isaacmao.com) (Chinese site) is a FVS for me (I just invented this abbr. FVS = Frequently Visited Site). His is among the first few bloggers in China. Later, he co-founded [cnblog.org](http://cnblog.org). Now, it is the gathering place for *academic* bloggers. By academic, I mean most contributors to cnblog.org are taking blogging serious and doing research on blogging as a technology and social phenomena.

There is always interesting stuff on his site, especially his [english site](http://meta.isaacmao.com/). It has a cool name META.

**Topku**

I never met Topku yet. [topku.com](http://topku.com) is a popular site. The nick name topku has an interesting story behind it - it should be read as To-PKU, where PKU means [Peking University](http://www.pku.edu.cn/). Topku is an active contributor to cnblog.org and a firm fighter against Fang Xing Dong's [blogchina.com](http://blogchina.com)

**Zheng**

Zheng is another famous guy that I wanted to meet for a long time. I read a lot on his blog [http://blogs.51.net/](http://blogs.51.net/) (Chinese site). His article quality is super good, with good thoughts, although I cannot understand (or have patient to think hard enough) all the posts ([example](http://blogs.51.net/archives/000732.html)).

**Other websites**

Here is some of my friend's blogs.

[Claire's Blog](http://www.blogcn.com/user6/claire/main.asp) - Claire was an intern here. She can speak French. She stayed in France for some time and worked (as an intern) in U.S. for some time. Her blog is among the most emotional blogs. There is another emotional blog [Chinese tea](http://christina.tecinese.com/). :-D

[Eddy](http://www.yinzirui.com) wrote a lot about his son. He uses his blog to record the days his son grows up. How lovely! The domain name yinzirui is the name of his son.

[Eric](http://blog.joycode.com/mvm) is a geek. This is what I wrote about him in [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/profile?viewProfile=&key=137113&fromSearch=0&sik=1086158355257&goback=%2Esrp_1_1086158355257)

> Zi Ying is among the smartest people I know in this world. He is a great developer with unexceptional knowledge of computer. He is also a high-tech geek who never stops explore new things. He owns blue tooth equipments and **changes his PDA more often than I change my tooth-brush**

What I said is true. He ran to me yesterday and show me his new SmartPhone. He changed his mobile again. Thanks to Eric that I am able to use the [GC75 GPRS Modem](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040428_sony_ericsson_gc75_in_shanghai.htm) so I could update this blog at home in the last month.

I have several geek friends in my friend cycle like Eric. [Xiao Gao](http://tanqi.com) is another. His girl friend is studying in Switzerland. He named his domain name by his girl friend's name - Tanqi.

He is the kind of guy who digs into the technical details. I am very surprised that he bought a IBM tablet-PC like equipment at only 800 RMB. The 14' LCD display comes with a PC inside, a touch screen and wireless LAN PC card. Now it is running Windows 98. Haha... He spent too much time on programming at [Tanqi.com](http://tanqi.com). Before Tanqi.com migrated to SPS, it has forum, wiki, blog with comment, pictures, font replacement functions, but with almost no articles, so with not many visitors. LOL.

[Frank](http://hippy.blogbus.com/) is of strong characteristics. He quitted Microsoft and joined a college to teach computer. He is going to Kanas this August. I am not sure if I can go there as I [planned](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030527_travel_plan_to_kanas_lake_xinjiang.htm).

[Grace](http://blog.joycode.com/grace) uses her blog as [part of her work](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040521_blogging_in_microsoft.htm). If she starts a personal blog one day, she will definitely become a good writer because she always has the latest news.

[run2me.com](http://run2me.com) is a continuously updating site (although not everyday). Check his trip to Paris. It is very nice. Run is so humorous that he [issued 5 website logos](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031128_expressing_with_icons.htm) to celebrate one month's opening of his site in Nov, the last year.

[Eddie](http://www.liuzhijian.com) told me excitedly that after I put [a link to his site](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040528_eddies_blog.htm), his site got 500 visitors in the following three days.
