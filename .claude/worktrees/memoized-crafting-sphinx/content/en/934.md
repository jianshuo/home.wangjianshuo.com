It is very nice to attend the [Shanghai Blogger Meetup](http://blog.meetup.com/216/?a=shareimg) organized by [Fons](http://www.chinaherald.net/). This meetup is held monthly on every third Wednesday @ 7:00 PM. This time, Trombly  kindly hosted us in thier home. I am not sure about whether I should disclose the full name of other participants yet. I had a great time. K and M came from the film industry. They make real films. I am very interested in joining their next film effort and see how a film is created. Matthew and others shared very interesting topics regarding blogging. I would like to recommend other bloggers to join the meetup. It is a very nice place to discuss about interesting stuff.
