Phil shared the new [Smart Card](http://www.flyingchair.net/story.php?storyID=786) version of his Hong Kong Permenant Identification Card on his[Flying Chair](http://www.flyingchair.net) website. It looks very cool. "I actually like the fact that I can put my driving license information on it and carry less cards. If you put it in a reader you can see all the information stored in the chip....", Phil mentioned. It is a cool feature. Now, I have at least three government issued cards I need to carry with me every day - the National ID (who knows when I need it), the [Driver's license](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20030821_passed_the_field_and_road_driving_exam.htm) (if I will drive) and the Social Insurance Card to see a doctor (good thing is, the expense will be covered by my insurance). It will be a good news if these cards can get together.

The Social Insurance Card I have already have some kind of IC card feature - I can go with the card and every hospital will recognize my name and dedute money from the card.

**101 Building in Taibei**

Thanks to Taipei101 MBlog website. There are plenty of pictures of the newly built 101 building in Taipei (via [CNBlog](http://www.cnblog.org/blog/archives/2003_11_16_cnblog_archive.html#106907244118550518))

![](/archives/2003/11/18/taipei-101.building-high.jpg)

Credit: [Taipei 101 Mblog](http://taipei101.textamerica.com)

It has 101 floors and took 5 years to complete. It is 88 meters higher (509m) than the Jinmao Tower (421m) in Shanghai and now ranks the tallest building in the world. This building looks similiar with Jinmao Tower from outside.
