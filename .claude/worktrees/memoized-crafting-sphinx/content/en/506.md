No only Shanghai has bad [taxi drivers](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20031202_watch_out_bad_shanghai_taxi_drivers.htm). My friends arrived in Beijing airport yesterday and took taxi to San Yuan Qiao. I know the destination is near and does not worth the long time waiting for the taxi driver (see more explaination in [this entry](http://home.wangjianshuo.com/archives/20040208_hello_from_beijing.htm)), but the taxi driver cheated them by going to the farther way.

The trip should cost 50 RMB at most, but the taxi driver asked for more than 100 RMB accordign to the meter.
